风险的动态演化How risk evolves

4.1 和 4.2 每天收盘对冲一次：用反向的股票把仓位的 delta 抵成 0（2.1 第五节），第二天再调一次。这一节讲两件事：调好的仓位为什么第二天就偏了；delta、gamma、theta、vega 自己会往哪边漂。数据是 2026-04-13 收盘 TSLA 的整条期权链，和 350 call 之后 24 天的真实报价；只改波动率的情形在市场上看不到，用 Black-Scholes 模型（2.1 用过的定价公式）算，图上注明。最后是讲义里一个四个希腊字母全为 0、风险却很大的仓位。4.1 and 4.2 hedge once a day at the close: offsetting stock brings the position's delta back to 0 (2.1, section 5), and the next day it is adjusted again. This node covers two things: why a position hedged today is off by the next day, and which way delta, gamma, theta and vega drift on their own. The data are the full TSLA option chain at the 2026-04-13 close and real quotes for the 350 call over the 24 days that follow. Cases where only volatility changes cannot be seen in the market; they are computed with the Black-Scholes model (the pricing formula used in 2.1) and labeled on the charts. Last comes a position from the lecture notes whose four Greeks are all 0 yet whose risk is large.

〇对冲好的仓位为什么会偏0Why a hedged position drifts

2.1 讲过 Δ：标的动 1 美元，期权跟着动多少。2.2 讲过 Γ：标的一动，delta 跟着变。标的不动，delta 也会变。2.1 covered Δ: how much the option moves when the underlying moves $1. 2.2 covered Γ: when the underlying moves, delta moves with it. Delta also changes when the underlying does not move.

决定一张期权价值的输入里，每天都在变的有三个：标的价格、离到期的时间、隐含波动率。delta 是用这三个输入算出来的，哪一个变了，delta 都会变。Of the inputs that set an option's value, three change every day: the underlying price, the time to expiration and the implied volatility. Delta is computed from these three inputs, and it changes when any of them changes.

The other two pieces are what this node is about: implied volatility fell 5.8 points and delta rose 0.027; a day passed and it rose another 0.004. Together the two offset a third of the price piece.

一波动率上升，delta 向 0.5 靠拢1Higher volatility pulls delta toward 0.5

同一个到期日（5/15，离到期 32 天）、同一个 TSLA 价格，只把隐含波动率从低调到高，整条 delta 曲线会怎样。市场上同一时刻只有一个报价，这件事只能用模型算。Same expiration (5/15, 32 days out), same TSLA price; only the implied volatility goes from low to high. What happens to the whole delta curve? The market shows only one quote at a time, so this can only be computed with a model.

2.1 的第四种读法：call 的 delta 粗略等于到期时落在行权价上方的可能性。1.2 讲过怎么量一个行权价离中心有几个标准差：The fourth reading from 2.1: a call's delta is roughly the chance it ends above the strike at expiration. 1.2 showed how to measure how many standard deviations a strike is from the center:

z ＝ ln(X ÷ F) ÷ (σ × √tz = ln(X ÷ F) ÷ (σ × √t)
行权价比远期价高出的比例，除以到期前的一个标准差。σ 变大，分母变大，同一个行权价离中心的标准差个数变少The fraction by which the strike is above the forward price, divided by one standard deviation before expiration. As σ grows the denominator grows, and the same strike is fewer standard deviations from the center

波动率升高，每个行权价都「离中心更近」了。深度实值的 call，本来几乎肯定落在行权价上方，现在没那么肯定，delta 从接近 1 往下掉；远虚值的，本来几乎不可能，现在有了机会，delta 从接近 0 往上升。两头都往中间挤，挤向 0.5。平值那一档本来就在中心，几乎不动。When volatility rises, every strike gets "closer to the center". A deep in-the-money call, once almost certain to end above its strike, is now less certain, and its delta drops from near 1; a far out-of-the-money one, once almost hopeless, now has a chance, and its delta rises from near 0. Both ends squeeze toward the middle, toward 0.5. The at-the-money strike was already at the center and barely moves.

By Black-Scholes (interest rate 0), at 4% and four weeks they are 0.0 and 100.0; at 40% and one year they are 53.1 and 62.9. 讲义旁边有一句手写批注：虚值 call 的 delta 到不了 50。这句话对「到期落在实值的概率」成立：行权价在远期价上方，到期落在它上方的可能性一定不到一半，这张 105 call 是 37.4%。2.1 第六节讲过，delta 比这个概率大，σ × √t 越大，大得越多；这里大了 15.7 个点，所以 Black-Scholes 的 delta 能到 53.1。A handwritten note beside the lecture example says an out-of-the-money call's delta cannot reach 50. That holds for "the chance of ending in the money": with the strike above the forward price, the chance of ending above it is always under half; for this 105 call it is 37.4%. As 2.1, section 6 showed, delta is larger than this probability, and more so the larger σ × √t is; here it is 15.7 points larger, so the Black-Scholes delta can reach 53.1.

中性是哪个波动率下的中性Neutral at which volatility

讲义的例子：一张 call 按 30% 的波动率算，delta 是 20。买 5 张，卖 1 张标的合约（delta 100），合计 5 × 20 − 100 ＝ 0，delta 中性。同一个仓位改用 35% 算，这张 call 的 delta 是 25，合计 5 × 25 − 100 ＝ +25。仓位一张没动，换了一个波动率，中性就不成立了。The lecture example: a call priced at 30% volatility has a delta of 20. Buy 5 and sell 1 underlying contract (delta 100): 5 × 20 − 100 = 0, delta neutral. Price the same position at 35% and the call's delta is 25: 5 × 25 − 100 = +25. Not a single contract changed; with a different volatility, the position is no longer neutral.

所以说一个仓位 delta 中性，要同时说用的是哪个波动率。讲义把用隐含波动率算出的 delta 叫 implied delta，同理还有 implied gamma、implied theta、implied vega。So saying a position is delta neutral also means saying which volatility was used. The lecture notes call the delta computed from implied volatility the implied delta, and likewise implied gamma, implied theta and implied vega.

二时间流逝，delta 离 0.5 更远2As time passes, delta moves away from 0.5

第一节的公式里，σ 和 √t 乘在一起：离中心几个标准差，只看 σ × √t 这一个乘积，也就是 1.2 的「到期前的一个标准差」。时间一天天过去，√t 变小，效果和波动率下降一样。所以时间对 delta 的作用和第一节相反：离 0.5 越来越远，实值的奔向 1，虚值的奔向 0。In the formula of section 1, σ and √t are multiplied together: how many standard deviations from the center depends only on the product σ × √t, the "one standard deviation before expiration" of 1.2. 三gamma 和 theta：平值与虚值反着走3Gamma and theta: at the money and out of the money go opposite ways

时间往前走，平值期权的 gamma 和 theta 越来越大，远虚值的越来越小。同一个原因，两边的结果相反。As time moves on, the gamma and theta of at-the-money options get larger and larger, and those of far out-of-the-money options smaller and smaller. One cause, opposite results.

到期分布在变窄The distribution at expiration narrows

2.2 讲过，gamma 是价值曲线的弯曲，最弯的地方在行权价附近。换一个看法：一张期权的 gamma，大致等于到期价格的分布在它的行权价上有多高。2.2 showed that gamma is the bend in the value curve, sharpest near the strike. Another way to see it: an option's gamma is roughly the height of the distribution of expiration prices at its strike.

理由用 2.1 的第四种读法：call 的 delta 粗略等于到期落在行权价上方的可能性。今天 TSLA 高 1 美元，整条到期分布跟着右移约 1 美元。原来落在行权价下方、离它不到 1 美元的那一小段，现在越过了行权价，delta 多出来的就是这一小段的可能性。一段 1 美元宽的可能性，约等于分布在那里的高度乘以 1。标的动 1 美元 delta 多出来的量就是 gamma，所以 gamma 约等于分布在行权价处的高度。行权价离 TSLA 越远，这个「约等于」差得越多，410 那里 gamma 比分布高度大三成多；但这个比例不随时间变，谁升谁降不受影响。The reason uses the fourth reading from 2.1: a call's delta is roughly the chance of ending above the strike. If TSLA is $1 higher today, the whole distribution at expiration shifts right by about $1. The small slice that used to end below the strike, less than $1 from it, is now past the strike, and the extra delta is the chance of that slice. The chance of a $1-wide slice is about the height of the distribution there times 1. The extra delta from a $1 move in the underlying is gamma, so gamma is about the height of the distribution at the strike. The further the strike is from TSLA, the rougher this "about" gets; at 410 gamma is over 30% larger than the distribution's height. But that ratio does not change with time, so it does not affect which one rises and which one falls.

1.2 讲过，到期分布的宽度按 √t 变化。离到期越近，分布越窄：中间越来越高，两边越来越薄。平值的行权价在中间，分到的越来越多；远处的行权价落在分布的尾部，那里的高度越来越低。1.2 showed that the width of the distribution at expiration scales with √t. The nearer expiration, the narrower the distribution: taller in the middle, thinner on both sides. An at-the-money strike is in the middle and gets more and more; strikes far away are in the tails, where the height keeps dropping.

讲义的两个例子Two lecture examples

讲义用 delta 的变化量 gamma：100 call 的 delta 是 50，标的从 100 涨到 102。离到期 1 天、低波动率时 delta 变成 90，gamma ＝ (90 − 50) ÷ 2 ＝ 20；离到期 1 年、高波动率时 delta 变成 60，gamma ＝ 5。临近到期、低波动率的平值期权，gamma 是另一张的 4 倍。The lecture notes use gamma as the change in delta: the 100 call has a delta of 50 and the underlying rises from 100 to 102. With 1 day to expiration and low volatility, delta becomes 90, gamma = (90 − 50) ÷ 2 = 20; with 1 year to expiration and high volatility, delta becomes 60, gamma = 5. Near expiration and at low volatility, the at-the-money option's gamma is 4 times the other one's.

讲义的定价网格（标的 100，波动率 25%，利率 0）把同一件事写成了价格。butterfly 是三个行权价的组合：买一张 95 call、卖两张 100 call、买一张 105 call。到期落在 100 时赚得最多（5 点），落在 95 以下或 105 以上一分不赚（3.2 另有详讲）。所以它的价格就是「到期落在中间附近」值多少。The lecture's pricing grid (underlying 100, volatility 25%, interest rate 0) writes the same thing as prices. 为什么不分岔Why it does not split

2.3 第四节讲过：σ 变 1 个点，分布宽度变 √t 个点，所以期限越短，每个行权价的 vega 都越小。这里把它和 gamma 放在一起比。2.3, section 4 showed that when σ changes 1 point, the width of the distribution changes √t points, so the shorter the term, the smaller the vega at every strike. Here it is compared with gamma.

先看平值 gamma 升得多快。分布宽度按 √t 变化，分布下面的总面积不变（所有可能加起来是 100%）：宽度缩到一半，中间的高度就升到 2 倍。所以中间的高度按 1 ÷ √t 变化，平值 gamma 也一样。时间剩四分之一，√t 剩一半，平值 gamma 变成 2 倍。First, how fast at-the-money gamma rises. The width of the distribution scales with √t, and the total area under it stays the same (all possibilities add up to 100%): when the width halves, the height in the middle doubles. So the height in the middle scales with 1 ÷ √t, and so does at-the-money gamma. With a quarter of the time left, √t is half, and at-the-money gamma doubles.

vega 和 gamma 之间有一个恒等关系：Black-Scholes 的 gamma 公式和 vega 公式里有同一个钟形高度因子，两者相除，这个因子消掉，剩下 S² × σ × t。本页不推导，用报价快照里的数核对：Vega and gamma are tied by an identity: the Black-Scholes formulas for gamma and for vega share the same bell-height factor; divide one by the other, the factor cancels, and S² × σ × t is left. This page does not derive it; it checks it with the numbers in the quote snapshot:

For strikes far from the money, gamma was already falling, and multiplied by a shrinking t, vega falls even faster.

讲义的例子：1 个月的期权，波动率 20% 时值 2.30，30% 时值 3.50，vega ＝ (3.50 − 2.30) ÷ 10 ＝ 0.12；3 个月的值 4.00 和 6.00，vega 0.20。0.12 × √3 ≈ 0.21，接近 0.20。The lecture example: a 1-month option is worth 2.30 at 20% volatility and 3.50 at 30%, so vega = (3.50 − 2.30) ÷ 10 = 0.12; a 3-month option is worth 4.00 and 6.00, vega 0.20. 0.12 × √3 ≈ 0.21, close to 0.20.

标的价格、行权价、远期价（1.1）。Underlying price, strike, forward price (1.1).
z
离中心几个标准差：ln(X ÷ F) ÷ (σ × √t)。How many standard deviations from the center: ln(X ÷ F) ÷ (σ × √t).
ln
自然对数。ln(X ÷ F) 约等于 X 比 F 高出的比例（1.2）。Natural logarithm. ln(X ÷ F) is about the fraction by which X is above F (1.2).
平值、实值、虚值At, in and out of the money
行权价在标的价附近、对持有人有利的一侧、不利的一侧。远虚值指离中心一个多标准差以外（第五节）。Strike near the underlying price, on the side that favors the holder, on the side that does not. Far out of the money means more than about one standard deviation from the center (section 5).
对冲、delta 中性Hedging, delta neutral
用反向的股票把仓位的 delta 抵成 0（2.1）。抵成 0 的仓位叫 delta 中性，只在算 delta 用的那个波动率下成立。Offsetting stock brings the position's delta to 0 (2.1). A position brought to 0 is called delta neutral, which holds only at the volatility used to compute delta.
Black-Scholes
期权定价公式。本页凡是只改一个输入的情形，都用它算，标为模型值。The option pricing formula. Every case on this page where only one input changes is computed with it and labeled a model value.
报价快照Quote snapshot
本页的真实报价、delta、gamma、theta、vega 都取自历史期权报价快照，每天 16:00 那一行（1.1）。The real quotes, delta, gamma, theta and vega on this page all come from historical option quote snapshots, the 16:00 row each day (1.1).
平滑隐含波动率Smoothed implied volatility
报价快照把各行权价的隐含波动率拟合成平滑曲线后，在某个行权价上的值；快照里的希腊字母用它算。The value at one strike on the smooth curve the quote snapshot fits through the implied volatilities of all strikes; the snapshot's Greeks are computed with it.
skew
同一到期日，不同行权价的隐含波动率不一样。TSLA 低行权价的更高：330 是 47.7%，390 是 44.9%。节点 8 细讲。Implied volatility differs across strikes with the same expiration. TSLA's lower strikes are higher: 47.7% at 330, 44.9% at 390. Node 8 covers it in detail.
implied delta
用隐含波动率算出的 delta。换一个波动率，同一个仓位的 delta 就不同。Delta computed from implied volatility. With a different volatility, the same position has a different delta.
一阶、二阶、三阶First, second and third order
期权价值随一个输入变多少是一阶（delta、theta、vega）；一阶的数再随某个输入变是二阶（gamma、vanna、charm）；再往下一层是三阶（color、zomma、speed）。How much the option's value changes with one input is first order (delta, theta, vega); how much a first-order number changes with an input is second order (gamma, vanna, charm); one more level down is third order (color, zomma, speed).
vanna, charm
delta 随波动率、随时间变多少。How much delta changes with volatility and with time.
color, zomma, speed
gamma 随时间、随波动率、随标的价格变多少。How much gamma changes with time, with volatility and with the underlying price.
vega decay, volga
vega 随时间、随波动率变多少。How much vega changes with time and with volatility.
butterfly
买一个低行权价、卖两个中间、买一个高行权价的组合（3.2）。价格就是到期落在中间附近值多少。Buy one low strike, sell two in the middle, buy one high strike (3.2). Its price is what ending near the middle at expiration is worth.
