提前行权Early exercise

5.2 留下一个问题：TSLA 450 上的 reversal（卖空股票、买 call、卖 put）表面上有利润，但卖出去的深度实值 put 可能被对手提前行权。这一节讲美式期权什么时候值得在到期前行权：股票 put 靠利息，股票 call 靠股息，期货期权靠内在价值的利息。案例是 TSLA 5/15 到期的深度实值 put 逐日报价，和 META 2026 年 6 月除息日前一天的 call。5.2 left a question open: the reversal on TSLA 450 (short stock, buy the call, sell the put) shows a profit on paper, but the deep in-the-money put that was sold may be exercised early by the other side. This node covers when an American option is worth exercising before expiration: stock puts on interest, stock calls on dividends, futures options on the interest on intrinsic value. The cases are daily quotes for deep in-the-money TSLA puts expiring 5/15, and META calls on the day before its June 2026 ex-dividend date.

〇行权与指派0Exercise and assignment

期权的买方可以要求按行权价成交，这个动作叫行权；卖方被选中履约，叫被指派。先把这两个动作里股票和钱怎么走弄清楚。The buyer of an option can demand a trade at the strike; that action is exercise. The seller chosen to deliver is assigned. First, sort out how the stock and the cash move in these two actions.

美式期权在到期日之前的任何一个交易日都可以行权，欧式期权只能在到期日行权。TSLA、META 这样的美股个股期权都是美式。一张合约对应 100 股（1.1）。An American option can be exercised on any trading day up to expiration; a European option only at expiration. US single-stock options such as TSLA and META are American. One contract covers 100 shares (1.1).

X 是行权价。讲义的练习：行权 4 张 May 75 call，得到 400 股，付出 30,000 美元；被指派 3 张 June 80 call，交出 300 股，收到 24,000 美元（讲义这一格手写成 $2,400，少了一个零）。账上走多少钱只看行权价，和当时的股价无关；股价决定的是这笔交易划不划算。X is the strike. The lecture's exercise: exercise 4 May 75 calls, get 400 shares and pay $30,000; be assigned on 3 June 80 calls, deliver 300 shares and receive $24,000 (the lecture notes write this cell by hand as $2,400, a zero short). How much cash moves depends only on the strike, not on the stock price at the time; the stock price decides whether the trade is a good one.

另外两种标的：期货期权的标的是一张期货，行权得到一份按行权价成交的期货头寸，第五节讲；指数期权行权不交割任何东西，只收付现金。Two other underlyings: the underlying of a futures option is a futures contract, and exercise delivers a futures position at the strike, covered in section 5; index options deliver nothing on exercise and settle in cash.

谁被指派：美国的清算所（OCC）把行权通知随机分给有空头仓位的券商，券商再按随机或先进先出分给具体客户。卖方事先不知道会不会轮到自己，只能按对手该不该行权来估计，第四节有真实的比例。Who gets assigned: the US clearing house (OCC) assigns exercise notices at random to brokers with short positions, and the brokers pass them on to specific customers at random or first in, first out. The seller does not know in advance whether it will be their turn and can only estimate from whether the other side should exercise; section 4 has real proportions.

兑现一张实值期权：卖掉还是行权Turning an in-the-money option into cash: sell it or exercise it

深度实值期权的买一经常低于内在价值，这时卖掉要少拿 84 美元。但这只回答了「怎么兑现」，没回答「该不该现在就兑现」：留到到期，这张 put 也能兑现内在价值。The bid of a deep in-the-money option is often below its intrinsic value; here selling gets $84 less. But that only answers "how to cash it in", not "whether to cash it in now": held to expiration, this put can also collect its intrinsic value.

讲义把「该不该提前」归结为一句话：只有持有标的比持有期权有好处，才值得提前行权。好处只有两种：现金能赚的利息，股票能拿的股息。没有股息、利率又是 0 的时候，美式期权从来不该提前行权，它和同样条件的欧式期权一样值钱。The lecture notes reduce "whether to exercise early" to one sentence: early exercise is worth it only if holding the underlying has an advantage over holding the option. There are only two advantages: the interest cash can earn and the dividends stock can collect. With no dividends and a zero interest rate, an American option should never be exercised early; it is worth exactly as much as a European option on the same terms.

一把期权价值拆开1Splitting the option value

讲义把美式期权的价值拆成内在价值加减三项。某一项大到把理论价值压到内在价值以下，就是提前行权的理由。The lecture notes split an American option's value into intrinsic value plus or minus three terms. When one term is large enough to push the theoretical value below intrinsic value, that is the reason to exercise early.

内在价值是现在行权能拿到的钱（1.1）。期权价格高出内在价值的部分叫时间价值。讲义把时间价值再拆成三块：Intrinsic value is the cash an exercise now would bring (1.1). The part of the option price above intrinsic value is time value. The lecture notes split time value further into three pieces:

C ＝ (S − X) ＋ X·r·t ＋ P − DC = (S − X) + X·r·t + P − D
call 的价值 ＝ 内在价值 ＋ 晚付行权价省下的利息 ＋ 同行权价 put 的价格 − 到期前的股息Call value = intrinsic value + interest saved by paying the strike later + price of the put at the same strike − dividends before expiration
P ＝ (X − S) − X·r·t ＋ C ＋ DP = (X − S) − X·r·t + C + D
put 的价值 ＝ 内在价值 − 晚收行权价少赚的利息 ＋ 同行权价 call 的价格 ＋ 到期前的股息Put value = intrinsic value − interest lost by receiving the strike later + price of the call at the same strike + dividends before expiration

两个式子都是 5.2 的 parity 移项：C − P ≈ S − X ＋ X·r·t − D。Both are the parity of 5.2 rearranged: C − P ≈ S − X + X·r·t − D.

Holding a call does not get them; exercising into stock does. A put holder who exercises delivers the stock; one who holds no stock delivers borrowed stock and becomes short stock, and has to pay the dividends on the borrowed shares.

保护的价值为什么正好是同一行权价、同一到期日的另一种期权？parity 里 C − P 只由 S、X、利息和股息决定，没有波动率。所以 call 价值里跟波动率有关的那部分，和同行权价的 put 一样多，两者的 vega 也相同（2.3）。讲义把这张相反类型的期权叫伴随期权。深度实值 call 的伴随 put 是虚值的，价格全部来自波动率。Why is the value of the protection exactly the other option with the same strike and expiration? In parity, C − P is set only by S, X, interest and dividends, with no volatility in it. So the volatility-related part of a call's value equals that of the put at the same strike, and the two have the same vega (2.3). The lecture notes call this opposite-type option the companion option. The companion put of a deep in-the-money call is out of the money, and its whole price comes from volatility.

三套判据Three tests

美式期权随时可以行权，所以价格不会低于内在价值，否则买进来马上行权就白赚。拆出来的理论价值一旦低于内在价值，持有到期就不如现在行权。这是提前行权的第一道判据，本页叫它寿命判据，因为它比的是从现在到到期的整段剩余寿命：An American option can be exercised at any time, so its price cannot fall below intrinsic value; otherwise buying it and exercising at once would be free money. Once the split-out theoretical value falls below intrinsic value, holding to expiration is worse than exercising now. That is the first test for early exercise; this page calls it the lifetime test, because it compares the whole remaining life from now to expiration:

寿命判据成立，只说明持有到期不如现在行权；是不是今天就行权，第二节再加一道只看明天的判据。call 的动力只有股息，put 的动力只有利息，利息和股息在两个式子里的位置正好对调。Passing the lifetime test only says that holding to expiration is worse than exercising now; whether to exercise today is decided in section 2 by a second test that looks only at tomorrow. A call's only motive is the dividend, a put's only motive is interest, and interest and dividend swap places between the two formulas.

期货期权没有股息，call 和 put 都靠利息。它的权利金有两种付法（2.3）：股票型结算是买期权当天全额付清，和股票期权一样；期货型结算是不预付，每天按价格变化结算盈亏。上表只对股票型结算成立，第五节细讲。Futures options have no dividends, so calls and puts both run on interest. Their premium can be paid two ways (2.3): stock-type settlement pays in full on the day the option is bought, as with stock options; futures-type settlement pays nothing up front and settles gains and losses daily as the price changes. The table above holds only for stock-type settlement; section 5 goes into detail.

股票 put：离到期 90 天时，股价低于 86.6 才落到内在价值以下，寿命判据成立；剩 30 天是 90.0，剩 1 天是 97.0。越近到期，伴随 call 越不值钱，分界越靠近行权价。Stock put: at 90 days to expiration, the stock has to fall below 86.6 before the put drops below intrinsic value and the lifetime test passes; with 30 days left it is 90.0, with 1 day left 97.0. The nearer expiration, the less the companion call is worth, and the closer the boundary moves to the strike.

落在这个区域里还不等于今天就该行权：美式 put 还能再等几天再行权，这段等待的价值欧式模型没有算。是不是今天行权，要看第二节的次日判据。用 5.2 的二叉树模型直接算美式 put 真正的行权边界，比这条分界更深：90 天约 80，30 天约 86，1 天约 96。剩 90 天、股价在 80 到 86.6 之间时，寿命判据成立，持有人仍然应该等。Landing in this region still does not mean exercising today: an American put can wait a few more days before exercising, and the European model leaves out the value of that wait. Whether to exercise today depends on the next-day test in section 2. Computing the true exercise boundary of the American put directly with the binomial tree model of 5.2 puts it deeper than this boundary: about 80 at 90 days, about 86 at 30 days, about 96 at 1 day. With 90 days left and the stock between 80 and 86.6, the lifetime test passes but the holder should still wait.

股票 call：明天的股息 1.00 要压过 100 × 6% × t。离到期超过约 61 天（单利粗算 1.00 ÷ (100 × 6%) × 365 ＝ 60.8 天，模型按连续复利是 61.1 天），利息一项就超过股息，任何价位都不该行权；剩 30 天时，股价 111.3 以上才该行权；剩 10 天是 103.9。这一栏的分界就是真正的行权点：明天除息之后，这张 call 再也没有提前行权的理由，不在今天行权就等于持有到期。Stock call: tomorrow's dividend of 1.00 has to outweigh 100 × 6% × t. With more than about 61 days to expiration (simple-interest estimate 1.00 ÷ (100 × 6%) × 365 = 60.8 days; the model, with continuous compounding, gives 61.1 days), interest alone exceeds the dividend, and the call should not be exercised at any price; with 30 days left, exercise only above 111.3; with 10 days left, above 103.9. In this column the boundary is the true exercise point: after tomorrow's ex-dividend, the call has no reason left for early exercise, so not exercising today is the same as holding to expiration.

期货 call（股票型结算）：深度实值时一定低于内在价值，剩 30 天时期货价 117.3 以上寿命判据成立；put 那一边对称。和股票 put 一样，真正的美式行权边界更远，二叉树算出剩 30 天时约 123。期货型结算：权利金不预付，没有利息可赚，曲线一处都不低于 0。Futures call (stock-type settlement): deep in the money it is always below intrinsic value; with 30 days left, the lifetime test passes above a futures price of 117.3, and the put side is symmetric. As with the stock put, the true American exercise boundary is further out: the binomial tree puts it at about 123 with 30 days left. Futures-type settlement: no premium is paid up front, there is no interest to earn, and the curve is nowhere below 0.

美式期权的价格不会低于内在价值，它比欧式多出来的，是将来走进这些区域时可以提前行权的权利。离这些区域越近，多得越多，但离得远也不是 0：5.2 用二叉树量过，4/13 的 450 put 多 0.94，还是虚值的 350 put 也多 0.08。An American option's price cannot fall below intrinsic value; what it has beyond a European option is the right to exercise early if it later walks into these regions. The closer it is to them, the more extra it is worth, but far away it is not 0 either: 5.2 measured it with the binomial tree, 0.94 extra for the 450 put on 4/13, and even the OTM 350 put had 0.08 extra.

今天还是明天Today or tomorrow

寿命判据成立只说明持有到期不如现在行权，没说今天就行权。今天行权比明天多赚一天利息 X·r ÷ 365，少了一天的保护价值，也就是伴随 call 一天的 Θ。讲义例 7：没有股息时，一天利息大于伴随 call 的 theta 才今天行权，否则等 call 自己再衰减一些。Passing the lifetime test only says holding to expiration is worse than exercising now; it does not say to exercise today. Exercising today instead of tomorrow earns one more day of interest, X·r ÷ 365, and gives up one day of protection value, the companion call's one-day Θ. Lecture example 7: with no dividend, exercise today only if one day's interest exceeds the companion call's theta; otherwise wait for the call to decay some more.

X·r·t ＞ C ＋ D　并且　X·r ÷ 365 ＞ |ΘC|X·r·t > C + D and X·r ÷ 365 > |ΘC|
整个剩余寿命里，利息多过保护与股息；只看明天这一天，利息也多过保护一天的衰减Over the whole remaining life, interest exceeds protection plus dividend; looking only at tomorrow, interest also exceeds one day's decay of the protection

前一条是第一节的寿命判据，后一条叫次日判据。两条都成立才今天行权；寿命判据成立而次日判据不成立，就再等。The first is the lifetime test of section 1; the second is called the next-day test. Exercise today only if both pass; if the lifetime test passes but the next-day test does not, wait.

470 put 卡在第二条上：利息 1.77 远大于 call 的 0.61，但一天利息 0.0554 小于 call 一天衰减 0.0590，再等。The 470 put is stuck on the second: interest of 1.77 is far larger than the call's 0.61, but one day's interest of 0.0554 is less than the call's one-day decay of 0.0590, so wait.

theta 是模型值：先从 call 中间价反推隐含波动率，再用 Black-Scholes 算每个日历日的 theta。报价快照里的 theta 列对深度虚值 call 偏小（470 call 是 −0.0376），和它自己的价格对不上，没有采用。Theta is a model value: first back out implied volatility from the call's mid, then compute each calendar day's theta with Black-Scholes. The theta column in the quote snapshot is too small for deep OTM calls (−0.0376 for the 470 call) and does not match their own prices, so it was not used.

五期货期权：靠内在价值的利息5Futures options: interest on intrinsic value

行权一张期货期权，得到一份按行权价成交的期货头寸，期货价与行权价之差当天记入账户。讲义 Session 2 的例子：3 月 S&P 500 期货 2150.00，行权 1 张 March 2100 call，得到 3 月期货多头。这张期货每点 250 美元，账户马上记入 (2150 − 2100) × 250 ＝ 12,500 美元。Exercising a futures option delivers a futures position at the strike, and the difference between the futures price and the strike is credited to the account that day. The example in Session 2 of the lecture: the March S&P 500 futures at 2150.00, exercise 1 March 2100 call, and get a long March futures position. This futures contract is worth $250 per point, so the account is credited (2150 − 2100) × 250 = $12,500 at once.

六判断流程6Decision steps

把前五节的判据排成一个流程。买方用它决定行不行权，卖方用它估计自己会不会被指派。The tests from the first five sections, arranged as one flow. The buyer uses it to decide whether to exercise; the seller uses it to estimate whether they will be assigned.

每一步都可能让结论停下来：虚值不行权；期货型结算不行权；寿命判据不成立就持有；时点不对就等到对的那天；次日判据不成立就再等一天看。Any step can stop the conclusion: out of the money, do not exercise; futures-type settlement, do not exercise; lifetime test fails, hold; wrong timing, wait for the right day; next-day test fails, wait another day and look again.

两个会改变结论的因素Two factors that change the conclusion

  • 隐含波动率下跌：伴随期权变便宜，放弃的保护变少，提前行权更可能。Falling implied volatility: the companion option gets cheaper, less protection is given up, and early exercise becomes more likely.
  • 手里的股票头寸：卖空股票收到的现金，券商付的利息（空头利率）低于正常利率。已经空着股票的人行权 call，只是平掉空头，放弃的利息更少，更愿意行权。行权 put 会变成空头的人，赚到的利息更少，更不愿意行权；手里本来有股票的人，行权 put 只是把股票按 X 卖掉，拿全额利率，最愿意行权。（讲义例 14）The stock position you hold: on the cash received from short selling, the broker pays interest (the short stock rebate) below the normal rate. Someone already short the stock who exercises a call is only closing the short and gives up less interest, so is more willing to exercise. Someone who would become short by exercising a put earns less interest and is less willing to exercise; someone who already holds the stock only sells it at X by exercising the put, earns the full rate, and is most willing to exercise. (Lecture example 14)

利息在 call 的判据里是成本，在 put 的判据里是收益，同一个事实对两边的影响相反。Interest is a cost in the call's test and a gain in the put's test; the same fact works in opposite directions on the two sides.

卖方的清单The seller's checklist

  • 卖出的股票 call：只在除息日前一天有提前指派的风险，按 D ＞ X·r·t ＋ P 估计；不派息就只会在到期时被指派。Short stock calls: the risk of early assignment exists only on the day before the ex-dividend date, estimated with D > X·r·t + P; with no dividend, assignment comes only at expiration.
  • 卖出的股票 put：深度实值、伴随 call 很便宜时，任何一天都可能被指派，除息日前的封锁期除外。Short stock puts: when deep in the money with a very cheap companion call, assignment can come on any day, except during the blackout period before ex-dividend.
  • 卖出的期货期权：股票型结算下，深度实值的 call 和 put 都可能被提前指派；期货型结算不会。Short futures options: under stock-type settlement, deep in-the-money calls and puts can both be assigned early; under futures-type settlement they cannot.

本页出现的全部符号与术语All symbols and terms on this page
行权Exercise
期权买方要求按行权价成交。call 行权是按 X 买入，put 行权是按 X 卖出。The option buyer demands a trade at the strike. Exercising a call buys at X; exercising a put sells at X.
指派Assignment
卖方被清算所随机选中履约。卖 call 被指派要交出股票，卖 put 被指派要买进股票。The seller is chosen at random by the clearing house to deliver. A short call that is assigned delivers stock; a short put that is assigned buys stock.
美式 / 欧式American / European
美式到期前任何交易日都能行权，欧式只能在到期日行权。American can be exercised on any trading day before expiration; European only at expiration.
S, X
标的价格、行权价。Underlying price, strike.
F
期货价格，期货期权的标的价。Futures price, the underlying price of a futures option.
r, t
年利率；从行权（或今天）到到期的时间，以年计。本页 r ＝ 4.3%（TSLA、META），讲义例子 6% 或 8%。Annual interest rate; time from exercise (or today) to expiration, in years. On this page r = 4.3% (TSLA, META); the lecture's examples use 6% or 8%.
D
到期前要除息的股息，每股。META 这次是 0.525。Dividend paid before expiration, per share. 0.525 for META this time.
C, P
同一行权价、同一到期日的 call 与 put 价格。Prices of the call and the put with the same strike and expiration.
X·r·t
利息价值：行权价这笔钱放 t 年的利息。call 行权损失它，put 行权赚到它。Interest value: the interest on the strike amount over t years. Call exercise loses it, put exercise earns it.
内在价值 / 时间价值Intrinsic value / time value
现在行权能拿到的钱；期权价格减内在价值。The cash an exercise now would bring; option price minus intrinsic value.
伴随期权Companion option
同一行权价、同一到期日、类型相反的期权。它的价格就是提前行权放弃的保护，也叫波动率价值。The option of the opposite type with the same strike and expiration. Its price is the protection given up by early exercise, also called volatility value.
Θ
theta，期权价值每过一天减少多少。次日判据用伴随期权一天的 theta。Theta: how much the option's value falls each day. The next-day test uses the companion option's one-day theta.
寿命判据Lifetime test
在整个剩余寿命里，提前行权得到的多过失去的，也就是持有到期不如现在行权。它成立还不等于今天就该行权。Over the whole remaining life, early exercise gains more than it loses; that is, holding to expiration is worse than exercising now. Passing it does not yet mean exercising today.
次日判据Next-day test
今天行权比明天行权多赚的一天利息，大于多失去的一天保护（伴随期权一天的 theta）。两条判据都成立才今天行权。The one day of interest gained by exercising today rather than tomorrow exceeds the one day of protection lost (the companion option's one-day theta). Exercise today only if both tests pass.
分界Boundary
模型里欧式理论价值正好等于内在价值的标的价格。put 在它下方、call 在它上方，寿命判据成立。真正的美式行权边界比它更深。The underlying price at which the model's European theoretical value exactly equals intrinsic value. Below it for a put, above it for a call, the lifetime test passes. The true American exercise boundary is deeper.
二叉树模型Binomial tree model
把到期前的时间切成很多小步，每步标的涨一格或跌一格，从到期往回算。美式期权每一步取「现在行权」和「继续持有」中大的那个（5.2）。Cut the time to expiration into many small steps, with the underlying moving up or down one notch at each step, and compute backward from expiration. At each step an American option takes the larger of "exercise now" and "keep holding" (5.2).
深度实值 / 虚值Deep in the money / out of the money
深度实值：内在价值很大，put 的行权价远高于股价，call 的行权价远低于股价。虚值：现在行权拿不到钱。Deep in the money: large intrinsic value, a put's strike far above the stock price, a call's strike far below it. Out of the money: exercising now brings nothing.
买一 / 卖一 / 中间价Bid / ask / mid
想买的人出的最高价；想卖的人要的最低价；两者的平均。马上卖出只能按买一，马上买入只能按卖一。The highest price buyers offer; the lowest price sellers ask; the average of the two. Selling right away gets only the bid; buying right away pays the ask.
清算所（OCC）Clearing house (OCC)
站在期权买卖双方中间、负责收付钱和履约的机构。美国股票期权由 OCC 清算，行权通知由它随机分给券商。The institution between option buyers and sellers that handles payments and performance. US stock options clear through the OCC, which assigns exercise notices to brokers at random.
股票空头 / 空头利率Short stock / short stock rebate
借来股票卖掉、以后买回还上；期间的股息由你补给出借人。卖空收到的现金，券商付的利率低于正常利率。Borrow stock and sell it, then buy it back later and return it; you pay the lender the dividends in the meantime. On the cash from the short sale, the broker pays a rate below the normal rate.
备兑卖 callCovered call
手里有股票，再卖出对应数量的 call。被指派时直接交出手里的股票。Holding the stock and selling the corresponding number of calls. When assigned, the shares on hand are delivered.
reversal
卖空股票、买 call、卖 put 的组合（5.2）。卖出的 put 深度实值时，可能被提前行权。Short stock, buy a call, sell a put (5.2). When the sold put is deep in the money, it may be exercised early.
除息日Ex-dividend date
第一个买了也拿不到这次股息的交易日。T+1 下与登记日同一天。The first trading day on which buying the stock no longer gets this dividend. Under T+1 it is the same day as the record date.
登记日Record date
公司按这一天收盘后的股东名册发股息。The company pays the dividend to the shareholders on its register after the close on this day.
封锁期Blackout period
除息日前 D × 365 ÷ (r × X) 天之内，不该行权 put。Within D × 365 ÷ (r × X) days before the ex-dividend date, a put should not be exercised.
期货 / 期货期权Futures / futures option
期货：现在约定价格、将来某天按这个价格买卖标的的合约，签约时不付货款，每天按结算价把盈亏划给对方（2.3）。期货期权：标的是一张期货的期权。Futures: a contract that sets a price now to buy or sell the underlying at that price on a future date, with no payment for the goods at signing and gains and losses paid to the other side every day at the settlement price (2.3). Futures option: an option whose underlying is a futures contract.
股票型 / 期货型结算Stock-type / futures-type settlement
权利金当天全额付清；权利金不预付、每天盯市结算。期货型结算的期权永远不提前行权。Premium paid in full on the day; premium not paid up front, marked to market daily. Options with futures-type settlement are never exercised early.
未平仓量Open interest
市场上还没了结的合约张数。行权和平仓都会让它减少。The number of contracts still open in the market. Exercise and closing trades both reduce it.
成交量Volume
当天成交了多少张合约。平仓一天最多让未平仓量减少这么多，超出的部分来自行权。How many contracts traded that day. Closing trades can reduce open interest by at most this much in a day; anything beyond that comes from exercise.
股息套利Dividend play
除息日前买股票、卖本该被行权的深度实值 call；没被指派就多拿一个股息。Before the ex-dividend date, buy the stock and sell deep in-the-money calls that should be exercised; if not assigned, collect an extra dividend.
