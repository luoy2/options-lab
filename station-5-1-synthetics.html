〇合成多头标的0Synthetic long underlying

讲义这一课（Session 5，11 页）没有一个定价公式，只讲一件事：几张合约放在一起，到期时等于另一张合约。先看最基本的一对。This lecture (Session 5, 11 pages) has no pricing formula. It covers one thing: several contracts held together equal another contract at expiration. Start with the most basic pair.

讲义这样写仓位：+1 December 100 call 是买入 1 张 12 月到期、行权价 100 的 call，−1 是卖出。一张期权对应一份标的合约。本页 TSLA 的例子里，标的就是 TSLA 股票，一张期权对应 100 股，下面的钱数都按每股算。The lecture notes write positions like this: +1 December 100 call means buying 1 call expiring in December with a strike of 100, and −1 means selling. 两个硬条件Two hard conditions

讲义特意写明：两条腿必须同一行权价、同一到期日。错开行权价，比如 350 call 配 330 put，330 到 350 之间两张都没人行权，这一段你既没买也没卖，线是平的，不再是标的。错开到期日，一张先到期、另一张还活着，先到期那天你手里只剩半个仓位。The lecture notes state it explicitly: the two legs must have the same strike and the same expiration. With different strikes, say a 350 call with a 330 put, between 330 and 350 neither is exercised, so over that range you neither buy nor sell, the line is flat, and it is no longer the underlying. With different expirations, one expires while the other is still alive, and on the first expiration day you are left holding half the position.

为什么说得通Why it works

买 put ＋ 买标的 ＝ 买 call。持有股票，再买一张 put 当保险：TSLA 跌破 350，put 把跌掉的部分补回来，损失停在 350；涨上去 put 作废，股票涨多少都归你。左边平、右边往上，就是一张 call 的形状。Long put + long underlying = long call. Hold the stock and buy a put as insurance: if TSLA falls below 350, the put makes up the loss and the loss stops at 350; if it rises, the put expires and every gain in the stock is yours. Flat on the left, rising on the right: the shape of a call.

Short the stock and buy a call as insurance: above 350, the call makes up the loss; if it falls, the call expires and every gain on the short is yours. delta 一正一负One delta positive, one negative

合成出来的 call 或 put，delta 在 0 和 ±1 之间；标的的 delta 是 1。所以用期权配标的时，两条腿的 delta 必须一正一负，互相抵掉一部分：买 put（负）配买标的（正），买 call（正）配卖标的（负）。两条腿同号，比如买 call 又买标的，delta 加起来绝对值超过 1，合成不出单张期权，也合成不出标的。它仍是一个正常的仓位（把标的换成 call − put，就是 2 张 call 减 1 张 put），只是不在上面这张表里。六节有两道题就是这种陷阱。A synthetic call or put has a delta between 0 and ±1, and the underlying's delta is 1. So when pairing an option with the underlying, the two legs' deltas must be one positive and one negative, partly canceling each other: a long put (negative) with long underlying (positive), a long call (positive) with short underlying (negative). If the two legs have the same sign, say a long call plus long underlying, their deltas add up to more than 1 in absolute value, and they cannot form a single option, nor the underlying. It is still a normal position (replace the underlying with call − put and it is 2 calls minus 1 put); it just isn't in the table above. Two questions in section 6 are this trap.

call 配 put 合成标的时正好反过来：买 call 为正，卖 put 也为正（卖出把 put 的负 delta 翻成正），两个加起来才能到 1。When pairing a call with a put to build the underlying, it is the other way around: a long call is positive, and a short put is positive too (selling flips the put's negative delta to positive); only together do they reach 1.

So call − put has a gamma and vega of 0, which means the call's and put's gammas must be equal, and their vegas too. 三拆3Splitting

讲义说：任何策略都可以用合成的办法做出来。替换本身只是查一节那张表；化简的关键是先决定哪几张配成一对去换，讲义管这一步叫「拆」。The lecture notes say any strategy can be built synthetically. The substitution itself is just a lookup in the table from section 1; the key to simplifying is deciding first which contracts to pair up and swap. The lecture notes call this step "splitting".

讲义第 7 页：两张 call，卖一份标的Lecture page 7: two calls, one short underlying

仓位是 +2 December 100 call，−1 标的合约。只把 call 换成 put 不行：+2 call ＝ +2 put ＋ 买 2 份标的，再加上原来卖出的 1 份，得到 +2 put、+1 标的。还是两行，只是从 call 的写法换成了 put 的写法，没有变简单。The position is +2 December 100 calls, −1 underlying contract. Just swapping the calls for puts doesn't work: +2 calls = +2 puts + buy 2 units of the underlying, and with the 1 unit already sold you get +2 puts, +1 underlying. Still two rows; it has only moved from call notation to put notation and has not become simpler.

能化出来的做法有两种。讲义的做法是先拆：两张 call 里拿出一张，和卖出的那份标的配成一对。买 call ＋ 卖标的，正是一节的合成 put；剩下那张 call 原样不动。另一种做法是换掉标的那一行：一节表里最后一行，卖出标的 ＝ 卖出 call ＋ 买入 put，换完是 +2 call、−1 call、+1 put，两行 call 合并成 +1。两种做法结果一样，都是 +1 call、+1 put：买入 December 100 straddle。There are two ways to simplify it. The lecture's way is to split first: take one of the two calls and pair it with the short underlying. Long call + short underlying is exactly the synthetic put from section 1; the other call stays as it is. The other way is to replace the underlying row: by the last row of the table in section 1, short underlying = short call + long put, which gives +2 calls, −1 call, +1 put, and the two call rows merge into +1. Both ways give the same result, +1 call and +1 put: a long December 100 straddle.

两种做法都先决定了哪几张配成一对：拆出一张 call 配标的，或者让标的自己换成一对期权，再和已有的 call 合并。只把两张 call 一起换，标的那一行消不掉，从 −1 变成 +1，还是两行。这就是讲义强调「拆」的原因。Both ways first decide which contracts to pair: split off one call to pair with the underlying, or turn the underlying itself into a pair of options and merge with the existing calls. If you swap both calls together, the underlying row doesn't go away; it goes from −1 to +1, and you still have two rows. This is why the lecture notes stress splitting.

牛市价差：call 版就是 put 版Bull spreads: the call version is the put version

讲义第 8 页：牛市价差 +1 December 90 call、−1 December 100 call，两条腿都换成 put：买 90 call ＝ 买 90 put ＋ 按 90 买入标的；卖 100 call ＝ 卖 100 put ＋ 按 100 卖出标的。标的一买一卖，张数对消，剩下 +1 90 put、−1 100 put，也是买低卖高，put 版的牛市价差。同行权价、同到期的 call 价差和 put 价差，是同一个东西。上面化简器的最后一个例子就是这一题。Lecture page 8: take the bull spread +1 December 90 call, −1 December 100 call and convert both legs to puts: long 90 call = long 90 put + buy the underlying at 90; short 100 call = short 100 put + sell the underlying at 100. One underlying bought and one sold, the quantities cancel, and what is left is +1 90 put, −1 100 put: also buying low and selling high, the put version of the bull spread. A call spread and a put spread with the same strikes and expiration are the same thing. The last example in the simplifier above is this exercise.

张数对消了，钱没有对消：按 90 买、按 100 卖，到期固定多出 10。所以 call 版 ＝ put 版 ＋ 到期那笔固定的 10。这回答了 3.1 留下的问题：TSLA 350/380 牛市 call 价差开仓付 11.975，牛市 put 价差开仓收 18.075，加起来 30.05，约等于间距 30。call 版多付的钱，买的就是到期那笔固定的 30；扣掉之后，两者 5/15 的盈亏是 +18.025 和 +18.075。30.05 为什么比 30 折回今天的 29.89 还多，3.1 讲过。The quantities cancel, but the money does not: buying at 90 and selling at 100 leaves a fixed extra 10 at expiration. So call version = put version + that fixed 10 at expiration. This answers the question 3.1 left open: the TSLA 350/380 bull call spread costs 11.975 to open and the bull put spread collects 18.075; together that is 30.05, about the width of 30. The extra money paid for the call version buys that fixed 30 at expiration; net of it, the two P&Ls on 5/15 are +18.025 and +18.075. Why 30.05 is even more than the 29.89 you get by discounting 30 to today was covered in 3.1.

化简的步骤Steps to simplify

  1. 选定一种期权，把另一种全部换掉：q 张 put 换成 q 张 call 加卖出 q 份标的；q 张 call 换成 q 张 put 加买入 q 份标的。哪一种行数少，就换掉哪一种。Pick one type of option and convert all of the other type: q puts become q calls plus selling q units of the underlying; q calls become q puts plus buying q units of the underlying. Convert whichever type has fewer rows.
  2. 按行权价合并同一种期权，张数相加。Merge options of the same type by strike, adding the quantities.
  3. 把所有标的腿加起来。Add up all the underlying legs.
  4. 标的合计不是 0 时，把剩下的标的也换掉：买 1 份标的 ＝ 买 1 张 call ＋ 卖 1 张 put，行权价用仓位里已有的那一个（和标的原来的成交价差出来的，只是到期一笔固定的钱），再合并一次，看能不能并成 straddle 这类已知结构。三节第 7 页的 +2 call −1 标的，把 −1 标的换成 −1 call +1 put，合并后是 +1 call +1 put。If the underlying total is not 0, convert the remaining underlying too: buy 1 underlying = buy 1 call + sell 1 put, using a strike already in the position (the difference from the underlying's original trade price is just a fixed amount at expiration). Merge again and see whether it forms a known structure such as a straddle. For the +2 calls −1 underlying on page 7 in section 3, replace −1 underlying with −1 call +1 put; after merging it is +1 call +1 put.
  5. 读出结构：butterfly、价差、ratio spread、straddle。Read off the structure: butterfly, spread, ratio spread, straddle.
  6. 每一步换完，到期价值那条线都不能变；变了，就是哪一步换错了。After each step, the expiration value line must not change; if it does, a step was done wrong.

delta 的变化速度；隐含波动率每变 1 个点的价格变化。标的的这两个数都是 0，所以同行权价同到期的 call 和 put 这两个数相同。How fast delta changes; the price change for each 1-point change in implied volatility. Both are 0 for the underlying, so a call and a put with the same strike and expiration have the same values for both.
拆Splitting
化简时先决定哪几张配成一对去换。可以从一行里拆出一张和标的腿配对，也可以把标的换成 call − put，再和已有的期权合并。When simplifying, decide first which contracts to pair up and swap. You can split one contract off a row to pair with the underlying leg, or replace the underlying with call − put and merge it with the existing options.
straddle
同行权价同到期，一张 call 加一张 put。Same strike, same expiration, one call plus one put.
strangle
同到期，一张低行权价的 put 加一张高行权价的 call。Same expiration, a lower-strike put plus a higher-strike call.
butterfly
三个等距行权价，+1 / −2 / +1。中间叫身，两边叫翼。到期价值在 0 和间距之间。Three equally spaced strikes, +1 / −2 / +1. The middle is the body, the outer ones the wings. Its value at expiration is between 0 and the width.
iron butterfly
身上的 straddle 与两翼的 strangle 一买一卖。买 straddle、卖 strangle 是买入（long）。A straddle at the body and a strangle at the wings, one bought and one sold. Buying the straddle and selling the strangle is the long side.
间距Width
相邻两个行权价之差。butterfly ＋ 买入 iron butterfly ＝ 间距。The difference between two adjacent strikes. Butterfly + long iron butterfly = width.
牛市价差Bull spread
同一种期权，买低行权价、卖高行权价（3.1）。call 版 ＝ put 版 ＋ 到期固定的间距。Same type of option, buy the lower strike, sell the higher strike (3.1). Call version = put version + the fixed width at expiration.
熊市价差Bear spread
同一种期权，买高行权价、卖低行权价，标的跌才赚（3.1）。随机题里会出现。Same type of option, buy the higher strike, sell the lower strike; makes money only if the underlying falls (3.1). Appears in the random exercises.
condor
四个等距行权价、同一种期权，+1 / −1 / −1 / +1，到期停在中间两个行权价之间时拿满（3.2）。随机题里会出现。Four equally spaced strikes, same type of option, +1 / −1 / −1 / +1; pays the full amount if expiration lands between the two middle strikes (3.2). Appears in the random exercises.
ratio spread（1×2）Ratio spread (1×2)
同一种期权、两个行权价，买卖张数不相等，1×2 是买 1 张、卖 2 张（3.2）。多出来的那几张决定哪一边亏损没有边界。Same type of option, two strikes, unequal quantities bought and sold; 1×2 means buy 1 and sell 2 (3.2). The extra contracts determine which side has unlimited loss.
box
两个行权价上一买一卖两个合成标的，到期固定拿到两个行权价之差。5.3 讲。Buying one synthetic underlying and selling another at two strikes; at expiration it pays the difference between the two strikes for sure. Covered in 5.3.
中间价Mid price
（买一 ＋ 卖一）÷ 2。买一是有人愿意买的最高价，卖一是有人愿意卖的最低价。(Bid + ask) ÷ 2. The bid is the highest price someone will pay; the ask is the lowest price someone will sell at.
买卖价差Bid-ask spread
卖一减买一。买入按卖一成交、卖出按买一成交，每条腿多付半个买卖价差（3.1）。深度实值期权的买卖价差宽，中间价的误差也大。Ask minus bid. Buys fill at the ask and sells at the bid, so each leg costs an extra half spread (3.1). Deep-in-the-money options have wide spreads, and their mid prices have large errors.
实值 / 虚值 / 深度实值In the money / out of the money / deep in the money
现在行权就值钱的叫实值，不值钱的叫虚值（3.2）。call 行权价低于现价是实值，put 行权价高于现价是实值。深度实值是离现价很远的实值。An option worth something if exercised now is in the money; one worth nothing is out of the money (3.2). A call is in the money when its strike is below the current price, a put when its strike is above it. Deep in the money means far from the current price.
盈亏平衡点Breakeven
到期时标的在这个价，仓位不赚不亏（3.1）。合成多头 350 是 350 ＋ 3.65 ＝ 353.65。The underlying price at expiration at which the position neither gains nor loses (3.1). For the 350 synthetic long it is 350 + 3.65 = 353.65.
折回（现值）Discounting (present value)
到期才拿到的钱，按利率算出今天值多少（1.1）。本页用 4.3%、单利：25 折 32 天是 24.91。Money received only at expiration, valued today using the interest rate (1.1). This page uses 4.3%, simple interest: 25 discounted for 32 days is 24.91.
