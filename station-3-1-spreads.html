价差家族The spread family

2.4 把买 350 call、卖 380 call 这个仓位压成了四个数，读出它剩下的几乎只有方向。这一节从这个仓位讲起：用同一到期日的两条腿表达看涨或看跌，把最大亏损和最大盈利都封住；再讲同样看涨的两个价差之间该选哪个。案例还是 2026-04-13 收盘的 TSLA 5/15 到期期权，5/15 收在 422.32。2.4 compressed the position "buy the 350 call, sell the 380 call" into four numbers and found that almost only direction was left. This node starts from that position: use two legs with the same expiration to express a bullish or bearish view, capping both the maximum loss and the maximum gain; then it covers how to choose between two spreads that are both bullish. The case is still TSLA options expiring 5/15 at the 2026-04-13 close; TSLA closed at 422.32 on 5/15.

〇第二条腿0The second leg

1.1 按卖一 21.10 买入这张 350 call（中间价 21.05），TSLA 涨多少都归你。如果你的看法只是「到 5/15 会涨到 380 附近」，380 以上那一段上涨对你没用，可以卖给别人，换回一部分成本。本页的成本统一按买一卖一的中间价算。1.1 bought this 350 call at the ask of 21.10 (mid 21.05), and however far TSLA rises, the gain is yours. If your view is only that TSLA "will rise to around 380 by 5/15", the part of the rise above 380 is no use to you, and you can sell it to someone else to get back part of the cost. Costs on this page are all at the bid-ask mid.

讲义给价差的定义是：在一个合约上持有头寸，同时在另一个合约上持有相反的头寸。最简单的一种只有两条腿：同一类型（都是 call 或都是 put）、同一到期日，只差行权价，一买一卖。报价单上同一到期日的行权价从上到下排成一列，两条腿在这一列里一上一下，所以叫垂直价差。The lecture notes define a spread as holding a position in one contract and, at the same time, an opposite position in another contract. The simplest kind has only two legs: the same type (both calls or both puts), the same expiration, differing only in strike, one bought and one sold. On a quote sheet the strikes of one expiration run down a single column, and the two legs sit one above the other in that column, which is why this is called a vertical spread.

4/13 收盘买 350 call，同时卖 380 call，就是一个垂直价差，写成 350/380：斜杠两边是两条腿的行权价，低的在前。这种买低行权价、卖高行权价的价差叫牛市价差（看涨时用），第一节讲全四种。卖出的 380 call 收 9.075，净付 21.05 − 9.075 ＝ 11.975。到期时它值多少：Buying the 350 call at the 4/13 close while selling the 380 call is a vertical spread, written 350/380: the two sides of the slash are the two legs' strikes, the lower one first. This kind of spread, buying the lower strike and selling the higher strike, is called a bull spread (used when bullish); section 1 covers all four kinds. Selling the 380 call collects 9.075, for a net payment of 21.05 − 9.075 = 11.975. What it is worth at expiration:

  • 在 350 和 380 之间：只有 350 call 有价值，价差值 S − 350。Between 350 and 380: only the 350 call has value, and the spread is worth S − 350.

和单买 350 call 比Compared with buying the 350 call alone

到期价在 380 以上时，单买 call 赚 S − 350 − 21.05，价差赚 18.025，两者在 S ＝ 389.075 处相等，正好是 380 加上卖出那张的价钱 9.075。到期价低于 389.075，价差都更好：少付的 9.075 在这一段一直留在手里。高于 389.075，单买 call 更好。With the expiration price above 380, the call alone makes S − 350 − 21.05 and the spread makes 18.025; the two are equal at S = 389.075, which is exactly 380 plus the 9.075 received for the sold call. Below an expiration price of 389.075 the spread is always better: the 9.075 not paid stays in your hand over that whole range. Above 389.075 the call alone is better.

So the probability of expiring below 389.075 is about 76% (the method in 1.2 puts the median at F). On 5/15 TSLA closed at 422.32, in the other 24%: the call alone made 51.27 per share at the mid (51.22 bought at the ask in 1.1), and the spread made 18.025.

By the method in 1.2, the z for 350 is ln(350 ÷ 353.69) ÷ 0.137 ≈ −0.08 and the z for 380 is ln(380 ÷ 353.69) ÷ 0.137 ≈ 0.52; in a bell curve 47.0% lies below −0.08, 30.0% above 0.52 and 23.0% in between, and these are the probabilities of the full loss, the full gain and the middle. The call alone also has a 47.0% probability of a full loss, only a bigger one (21.05); on the winning side it has no cap: 69 of the 600 balls are above the actual 5/15 close of 422.32, and at those prices the call alone makes 51.27 or more per share. The spread sells off the rise above 380 in exchange for a lower cost and a smaller maximum loss.

一四种垂直价差1Four vertical spreads

讲义的定义：牛市价差（bull spread）买低行权价、卖高行权价；熊市价差（bear spread）买高行权价、卖低行权价。两条腿必须同一类型、同一到期日。call 和 put 各能做一种，一共四种。The lecture notes' definitions: a bull spread buys the lower strike and sells the higher strike; a bear spread buys the higher strike and sells the lower strike. Both legs must be the same type with the same expiration. Calls and puts can each make one of each, four in all.

「买低卖高」为什么对 call 和 put 都是看涨？讲义有一句：行权价低的 call，delta 总是更大。put 也是行权价低的 delta 更大：4/13 的 350 put 是 −0.44，380 put 是 −0.68，350 那张负得少。买 delta 大的、卖 delta 小的，合起来是正 Δ，TSLA 涨就赚。四个价差都用 4/13 收盘的 350 和 380：Why is "buy low, sell high" bullish for both calls and puts? The lecture notes have a line: the call with the lower strike always has the larger delta. For puts, the lower strike also has the larger delta: on 4/13 the 350 put was −0.44 and the 380 put −0.68, so the 350 is less negative. Buy the larger delta and sell the smaller one, and the combination has positive Δ, making money when TSLA rises. All four spreads use the 350 and 380 at the 4/13 close:

两个牛市价差的形状一样：350 以下亏满，380 以上赚满，盈亏平衡都在 362 附近（call 版 361.975，put 版 361.925）。区别在开仓时的现金。call 版买的那张贵，要付钱，叫借方价差；put 版卖的那张贵（380 put 已经是实值），开仓就收 18.075，叫贷方价差。收钱不等于赚钱：TSLA 到期跌破 350，两张 put 都实值，要净赔 30，扣掉收的 18.075，亏 11.925。The two bull spreads have the same shape: the full loss below 350, the full gain above 380, and break-even near 362 for both (361.975 for the call version, 361.925 for the put version). The difference is the cash at opening. In the call version the leg bought is the more expensive one, so you pay, and it is called a debit spread; in the put version the leg sold is the more expensive one (the 380 put is already in the money), so opening collects 18.075, and it is called a credit spread. Collecting money is not making money: if TSLA expires below 350, both puts are in the money and owe a net 30; minus the 18.075 collected, that is a loss of 11.925.

5/15 TSLA 收在 422.32，两个牛市价差都赚满：call 版 +18.025，put 版 +18.075，每张约 1,800 美元，两者只差 0.05。两个熊市价差正好相反，亏满。同样两个行权价的 call 价差和 put 价差为什么几乎是同一个东西，5.1 用合成关系讲。On 5/15 TSLA closed at 422.32, and both bull spreads made their full gain: +18.025 for the call version and +18.075 for the put version, about $1,800 per contract, only 0.05 apart. The two bear spreads are exactly the opposite, with the full loss. Why a call spread and a put spread at the same two strikes are almost the same thing is covered in 5.1 with synthetic relationships.

这里有一处对不上。第二节第三条会讲：同一对行权价的 call 价差加 put 价差，应当等于间距折回今天的钱，30 按 4.3% 折 32 天是 29.89。按中间价，11.975 ＋ 18.075 ＝ 30.05，反而比 30 多。一个原因是买卖价差：真按报价成交，两个都买要付 12.05 ＋ 18.25 ＝ 30.30，两个都卖只收 11.90 ＋ 17.90 ＝ 29.80，29.89 在这两个数中间。另一个原因在 380 put 上：它比按 1.1 的 put-call parity 算出的欧式价格贵 0.19，350 put 只贵 0.03。TSLA 期权是美式的，实值的 put 可以提前行权，所以比欧式的贵，节点 9 讲。There is one thing here that does not line up. Section 2's third rule will say that a call spread plus a put spread at the same pair of strikes should equal the strike interval discounted back to today: 30 discounted for 32 days at 4.3% is 29.89. At mid prices, 11.975 + 18.075 = 30.05, more than 30. One reason is the bid-ask spread: actually trading at quotes, buying both costs 12.05 + 18.25 = 30.30, and selling both collects only 11.90 + 17.90 = 29.80, with 29.89 between the two. The other reason is in the 380 put: it is 0.19 more expensive than the European price from the put-call parity of 1.1, while the 350 put is only 0.03 more. TSLA options are American, and an in-the-money put can be exercised early, so it is worth more than a European one; node 9 covers this.

按买一卖一成交，每个价差都要多付两条腿各半个买卖价差。call 版两条腿的买卖价差是 0.10 和 0.05，多付 0.075；put 版是 0.10 和 0.25，多付 0.175。形状一样的两个版本，实际交易时挑买卖价差窄的那一个。Trading at the bid and ask, every spread costs an extra half bid-ask spread on each of its two legs. The call version's legs have bid-ask spreads of 0.10 and 0.05, an extra 0.075; the put version's are 0.10 and 0.25, an extra 0.175. Of two versions with the same shape, trade the one with the narrower bid-ask spreads.

三条规律Three rules

  1. 每一格都不超过两个行权价之差。90/95 的 call 价差 3.90，小于 5；90/110 是 10.40，小于 20。No cell exceeds the difference between its two strikes. The 90/95 call spread is 3.90, less than 5; 90/110 is 10.40, less than 20.
  2. 间距相同，越靠实值越贵。间距 5 的 call 价差：90/95 是 3.90，95/100 是 3.10，100/105 是 2.10，105/110 是 1.30。put 价差反过来，105/110 最贵，3.60。For the same interval, the closer to in the money, the more expensive. Call spreads with an interval of 5: 90/95 is 3.90, 95/100 is 3.10, 100/105 is 2.10, 105/110 is 1.30. Put spreads run the other way, with 105/110 the most expensive at 3.60.
  3. 同一对行权价，call 价差加 put 价差是个定数。间距 5 的四对都是 4.90，间距 10 的三对都是 9.80，间距 20 的是 19.60，都是间距的 98%。For the same pair of strikes, the call spread plus the put spread is a fixed amount. All four pairs with an interval of 5 total 4.90, all three with an interval of 10 total 9.80, and the one with 20 totals 19.60, each 98% of the interval.

第三条：同一对行权价，比如 95/100，到期在 100 以上，call 价差值 5、put 价差值 0；在 95 以下反过来；在 95 和 100 之间，call 价差值 S − 95，put 价差值 100 − S，加起来还是 5。两个一起买，不管标的到期在哪都拿到 5 美元，所以两个价格之和就是「到期稳拿 5 美元」的价格。表里是 4.90 不是 5.00：这 5 美元要到期才拿到，这张表按约 2% 折了现。这个组合叫 box，5.3 讲。The third rule: for one pair of strikes, say 95/100, above 100 at expiration the call spread is worth 5 and the put spread 0; below 95 it is the reverse; between 95 and 100 the call spread is worth S − 95 and the put spread 100 − S, which still add up to 5. Buy both, and wherever the underlying ends up you get $5, so the sum of the two prices is the price of "$5 for certain at expiration". The table shows 4.90, not 5.00: the $5 arrives only at expiration, and this table discounts them by about 2%. This combination is called a box, covered in 5.3.

With a rate above 0, C − P would be a bit larger than S0 − X (1.1); here they are exactly equal, which means a rate of 0. Recomputed with Black-Scholes (rate 0, 6 weeks as 42 days), none of the 18 prices differs from the lecture notes by more than 0.005; the deltas come out at 78.5, 51.4 and 24.7, which the lecture notes rounded to whole numbers.

平值腿在买方还是卖方Is the at-the-money leg bought or sold?

讲义这一页的要点就是这句话。平值腿指两条腿里行权价离标的价最近的那一条。它的 vega 最大，gamma、theta 的绝对值也最大（2.2、2.3），整个价差的这三个数跟着它的正负号走：This sentence is the point of this page of the lecture notes. The at-the-money leg is whichever of the two legs has its strike closest to the underlying price. It has the largest vega, and also the largest gamma and theta in absolute value (2.2, 2.3), so these three numbers of the whole spread follow its sign:

put 版一样。讲义的 put 表：95/100 put 价差（买 100 put、卖 95 put，熊市）买入了平值腿，波动率从 15% 到 25%，从 1.62 涨到 2.00；100/105 put 价差（买 105 put、卖 100 put）卖出了平值腿，从 3.44 降到 3.14。同一列里 call 价差加 put 价差都是 5.00：利率为 0，不用折现，这就是上一节的第三条。Puts work the same way. In each column the call spread plus the put spread is 5.00: with a rate of 0 there is no discounting, and this is the third rule from the previous section.

这条规则要求平值腿明显比另一条腿靠近标的价。同一天 vega 最大的行权价略高于标的价（4/13 TSLA 352.36，vega 最大的是 355），gamma 也是这样。标的走到两腿中点附近时，上方那条腿的 vega 会先超过下方那条，价差的 vega 在标的越过中点之前就翻号（第五节的 4/14）。这时要直接比两条腿的 vega。This rule requires the at-the-money leg to be clearly closer to the underlying price than the other leg. On a given day the strike with the largest vega is slightly above the underlying price (on 4/13 TSLA was 352.36 and the largest vega was at 355), and the same goes for gamma. When the underlying gets near the midpoint between the two legs, the upper leg's vega overtakes the lower leg's first, and the spread's vega flips sign before the underlying crosses the midpoint (4/14 in section 5). Then compare the two legs' vegas directly.

四双判据：先定方向，再看平值腿4Two criteria: direction first, then the at-the-money leg

讲义作业里有一类题：给出标的价、方向看法和对隐含波动率的看法，从四个价差里挑一个。挑法分两步，方向先筛掉一半，vega 再从剩下的里挑。The lecture notes' homework has a type of question: given the underlying price, a directional view and a view on implied volatility, choose one of four spreads. The choice takes two steps: direction removes half, and vega picks from what is left.

第一步　方向Step 1 Direction

看涨选牛市价差（买低卖高），看跌选熊市价差（买高卖低）。call 版、put 版都算。If bullish, choose a bull spread (buy low, sell high); if bearish, a bear spread (buy high, sell low). Call and put versions both count.

第二步　平值腿Step 2 At-the-money leg

认为隐含波动率偏高，要负 vega，选平值腿在卖方的那个；认为偏低，要正 vega，选平值腿在买方的那个。If you think implied volatility is too high, you want negative vega: choose the one with the at-the-money leg sold. If you think it is too low, you want positive vega: choose the one with the at-the-money leg bought.

第二步问的是隐含波动率偏高还是偏低，这要靠你对未来波动率的判断（1.2：隐含波动率是价格，未来波动率是价值），数据本身给不出答案。例如 4/13 这天，5/15 到期的期权里包含了 4/22 收盘后的财报（2.3），隐含波动率里有一部分是在为财报那一下跳动付钱。财报之后隐含波动率会降，这一点市场事先就知道，已经算进了价格；认为那一下跳动会比市场定价的小的人，才会认为隐含波动率偏高。财报公布后，4/23 那天 350 call 的隐含波动率从 48.55% 降到 43.16%（这是 350 call 自己的；2.3 用离标的最近的行权价上 call 与 put 的平均，是 48.1% 降到 41.9%）。Step 2 asks whether implied volatility is too high or too low, which depends on your judgment of future volatility (1.2: implied volatility is the price, future volatility is the value); the data alone cannot answer it. For example, on 4/13 the options expiring 5/15 included the earnings release after the 4/22 close (2.3), and part of the implied volatility was paying for the jump at earnings. That implied volatility would fall after earnings was known to the market in advance and already in the price; only someone who thinks the jump will be smaller than the market has priced would consider implied volatility too high. After the release, on 4/23 the 350 call's implied volatility fell from 48.55% to 43.16% (this is the 350 call's own; 2.3 used the average of the call and put at the strike nearest the underlying, which fell from 48.1% to 41.9%).

call、put 的价格。利率为 0 时，同一行权价满足 C − P ＝ S0 − X（1.1）。Prices of the call and the put. With a rate of 0, the same strike satisfies C − P = S0 − X (1.1).
t
离到期的时间，按年算。4/13 到 5/15 是 32/365。Time to expiration in years. 4/13 to 5/15 is 32/365.
ln
自然对数，e 的反运算。ln(X/F) 是 X 比 F 高出的比例（1.2）。Natural logarithm, the inverse of e. ln(X/F) is the proportion by which X is above F (1.2).
max(a, 0)
a 为负时取 0。期权到期的价值写成 max(S − X, 0)（1.1）。0 when a is negative. An option's value at expiration is written max(S − X, 0) (1.1).
平值腿At-the-money leg
两条腿里行权价离标的价最近的那一条。一般它的 vega、gamma、theta 最大，价差的这三个数跟着它的正负号走。vega 最大的行权价略高于标的价，标的走到两腿中点附近时要直接比两条腿的 vega。Whichever of the two legs has its strike closest to the underlying price. It usually has the largest vega, gamma and theta, and these three numbers of the spread follow its sign. The strike with the largest vega is slightly above the underlying price, so when the underlying is near the midpoint of the two legs, compare the two legs' vegas directly.
Δ
delta，TSLA 涨 1 美元价格变多少；一张价差按股计（2.1、2.4）。Delta: when TSLA rises $1, how much the price changes; for one spread, in shares (2.1, 2.4).
ν, vega
隐含波动率涨 1 个点，价格变多少（2.3）。价差的 vega ＝ 买入腿的 vega − 卖出腿的 vega。When implied volatility rises 1 point, how much the price changes (2.3). Spread vega = vega of the leg bought − vega of the leg sold.
gamma, theta
delta 变得多快、每天的时间价值流失（2.2）。How fast delta changes, and the time value lost each day (2.2).
σ
年化波动率；隐含波动率是从期权价格反推出来的 σ（1.2）。Annualized volatility; implied volatility is the σ backed out from option prices (1.2).
F
远期价，分布的中心（1.1）。TSLA 本例 353.69。Forward price, the center of the distribution (1.1). 353.69 for TSLA in this case.
z
离中心几个标准差，z ＝ ln(X/F) ÷ (σ√t)（1.2）。How many standard deviations from the center, z = ln(X/F) ÷ (σ√t) (1.2).
中间价Mid
买一与卖一的平均。实际成交时买在卖一、卖在买一，每条腿多付半个买卖价差。The average of the bid and the ask. In actual trading you buy at the ask and sell at the bid, paying an extra half bid-ask spread on each leg.
盈亏平衡Break-even
到期盈亏正好为 0 的标的价。350/380 牛市 call 价差是 350 ＋ 11.975 ＝ 361.975。The underlying price at which the P&L at expiration is exactly 0. For the 350/380 bull call spread it is 350 + 11.975 = 361.975.
box
同一对行权价的 call 价差加 put 价差，到期稳拿间距那么多钱（5.3）。A call spread plus a put spread at the same pair of strikes pays exactly the strike interval at expiration (5.3).
