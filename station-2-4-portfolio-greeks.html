组合希腊字母Portfolio Greeks

2.1 到 2.3 讲的都是一张期权自己的 delta、gamma、theta、vega。真实的仓位常常有好几条腿：几张 call、几张 put，再加一些股票。这一节把各条腿的希腊字母加起来，压成四个数，用它们读出仓位在赌什么，再估算一次市场变动会赚亏多少。所有仓位都用 2026-04-13 收盘 TSLA 的真实报价搭。2.1 to 2.3 covered the delta, gamma, theta and vega of a single option. Real positions often have several legs: a few calls, a few puts, plus some stock. This node adds up the Greeks of all the legs into four numbers, uses them to read what the position is betting on, and then estimates how much a market move will make or lose. Every position is built from real TSLA quotes at the 2026-04-13 close.

〇先按仓位算0First, count per position

买 1 张 350 call，这条腿的四个数是：Buy one 350 call, and this leg's four numbers are:

  • delta 55.74：相当于持有 55.74 股 TSLA，就是 2.1 讲的等价标的头寸。delta 55.74: equivalent to holding 55.74 shares of TSLA, the equivalent underlying position from 2.1.
  • gamma 0.82：TSLA 每涨 1 美元，这 55.74 股再多 0.82 股。gamma 0.82: for each 1-dollar rise in TSLA, these 55.74 shares grow by another 0.82 shares.
  • theta −31.90：别的都不变，每过一天少 31.90 美元。theta −31.90: with everything else unchanged, each day that passes loses 31.90 dollars.
  • vega 41.25：隐含波动率每涨 1 个点，多 41.25 美元。vega 41.25: each 1-point rise in implied volatility adds 41.25 dollars.

卖出同一张，四个数的符号全部翻过来：−55.74、−0.82、+31.90、−41.25。Sell the same contract and all four signs flip: −55.74, −0.82, +31.90, −41.25.

股票也可以当成一条腿。一股 TSLA 的 delta 是 1，gamma、theta、vega 都是 0：股票的价值跟着股价一比一地变，不会弯；不会随时间衰减；和隐含波动率无关。买 11 股，这条腿就是 delta 11，其余三个数都是 0。Stock can be a leg too. One share of TSLA has a delta of 1 and gamma, theta and vega of 0: a share's value moves one-for-one with the stock price, with no bend; it does not decay over time; and it has nothing to do with implied volatility. Buy 11 shares, and that leg is delta 11 with the other three numbers 0.

报价快照里的 theta 只有 call 的一列。put 的 theta 用 1.1 的 put-call parity 换算。1.1 写的是 C − P ＝ (F − X) ÷ (1 ＋ r·t)：同一行权价、同一到期日的 call 价格减 put 价格，等于远期价减行权价再折回今天。TSLA 不分红，这就是 S 减去行权价 X 折回今天的值。过一天，行权价折回今天的值多出约 r × X ÷ 365，call − put 就少这么多，所以 put 的 theta 比 call 高出这一块（r ＝ 4.3%，350 put 是 −0.278 每天，call 是 −0.319）。同一个式子的右边对 S 是一条直线、和隐含波动率无关，所以 call 与 put 的 gamma、vega 相同。The quote snapshot has only one theta column, for calls. A put's theta is converted with the put-call parity from 1.1. 1.1 wrote it as C − P = (F − X) ÷ (1 + r·t): the call price minus the put price at the same strike and expiration equals the forward price minus the strike, discounted back to today. TSLA pays no dividend, so this is S minus the strike X discounted back to today. After one day, the strike's value discounted back to today grows by about r × X ÷ 365, so call − put shrinks by that much, and the put's theta is higher than the call's by that piece (r = 4.3%; the 350 put is −0.278 a day, the call −0.319). The right side of the same equation is a straight line in S and does not depend on implied volatility, so a call and a put have the same gamma and vega.

第五个希腊字母 ρ（rho，2.3）在一个月的期权上很小：1 张 350 call 的 rho 是 15.45 美元每个百分点，而利率一个月里很少动一个百分点。本页只用四个数。The fifth Greek, ρ (rho, 2.3), is small on a one-month option: one 350 call has a rho of 15.45 dollars per percentage point, and interest rates rarely move a full percentage point in a month. This page uses only four numbers.

一希腊字母可以直接相加1Greeks can simply be added

一个仓位的价值是各条腿价值的和。所以整个仓位的 delta 就是各条腿 delta 的和，gamma、theta、vega 也一样。A position's value is the sum of its legs' values. So the delta of the whole position is the sum of the legs' deltas, and the same goes for gamma, theta and vega.

每个希腊字母问的都是同一类问题：某一个量动一点，价值变多少。delta 问 TSLA 涨 1 美元，theta 问过一天，vega 问隐含波动率涨 1 个点，gamma 问的是 delta 自己。仓位价值 ＝ 第一条腿 ＋ 第二条腿 ＋ …。TSLA 涨 1 美元时，每条腿各变各的，仓位的变化就是这些变化加起来。Every Greek asks the same kind of question: when one quantity moves a little, how much does the value change? Delta asks about TSLA rising 1 dollar, theta about one day passing, vega about implied volatility rising 1 point, and gamma asks about delta itself. Position value = first leg + second leg + …. When TSLA rises 1 dollar, each leg changes on its own, and the position's change is those changes added up.

Δ仓位 ＝ Δ1 ＋ Δ2 ＋ … ＋ ΔnΔport = Δ1 + Δ2 + … + Δn
仓位的 delta ＝ 每条腿的 delta（已经乘了 100 × 张数、带正负号）之和；Γ、Θ、ν 同样The position's delta = the sum of each leg's delta (already multiplied by 100 × contracts and signed); likewise for Γ, Θ and ν

直接相加有一个前提：每条腿量的是同一个标的、同一种单位，都是 TSLA 的每 1 美元、每 1 天、每 1 个波动率点。vega 相加还多一层默认：各条腿的隐含波动率一起涨跌同样多的点。同一到期日、行权价相近的腿大致如此；到期日不同的腿不一定。Adding them directly assumes that every leg measures the same underlying in the same units: per 1 dollar of TSLA, per 1 day, per 1 volatility point. Adding vegas carries one more assumption: that every leg's implied volatility moves up or down by the same number of points. That roughly holds for legs with the same expiration and nearby strikes; for legs with different expirations it may not.

One call and one put with the same strike and the same expiration are called a straddle. Sell the 350 call, delta −55.74; then sell the 350 put, whose delta is negative to begin with, so selling it gives +44.26, for a total of −11.48; then buy 11 shares, and delta becomes −0.48, almost 0. When the second leg goes in, gamma and vega double, and theta goes from 31.90 to 59.69, nearly double (the put's theta is a bit smaller); buying the stock leaves all three numbers untouched: stock can only adjust delta.

牛市价差。买 350 call、卖 380 call，叫牛市价差。卖出 380 call 之后，delta 从 55.74 降到 23.91，还留着 43%；gamma、theta、vega 只留下 7%、14%、9%。两张期权离现价都不远，gamma、theta、vega 差不多大（gamma 一个 0.0082、一个 0.0076），delta 却差得多（0.5574 对 0.3183）。相减之后，剩下的几乎只有方向。Bull spread. Buying the 350 call and selling the 380 call is called a bull spread. After selling the 380 call, delta drops from 55.74 to 23.91, keeping 43%; gamma, theta and vega keep only 7%, 14% and 9%. Both options are close to the current price, so their gamma, theta and vega are similar (gamma 0.0082 and 0.0076), but their deltas differ a lot (0.5574 against 0.3183). After subtracting, what is left is almost only direction.

七条腿。两个到期日，call 和 put，买的卖的都有，再加 60 股。一条条加上去，四个数来回摆，最后停在 delta +45.46、gamma −1.13、theta +42.72、vega −50.96。只看这七条腿，很难说它在赌什么；看这四个数就能说出来，下一节讲怎么读。Seven legs. Two expirations, calls and puts, bought and sold, plus 60 shares. Adding them one at a time, the four numbers swing back and forth and finally settle at delta +45.46, gamma −1.13, theta +42.72, vega −50.96. From the seven legs alone it is hard to say what this bets on; the four numbers tell you, and the next section covers how to read them.

delta 和 gamma 连起来读Reading delta and gamma together

delta 说方向，gamma 说 delta 跟着价格变得多快。delta 为正、gamma 为负的仓位看涨，但 TSLA 每涨一段，它的 delta 就小一点：涨得越多（离入场价越远），后面那一段赚得越少。跌的时候反过来，delta 越跌越大，亏得越来越快。仓位不调整时，gamma 带来的这部分损失只看 TSLA 从入场价一共走了多远，不管分几天走完（第三节会算出来）。所以它要的是慢慢涨：同样涨到那里，用的天数越多，一路按天收的 theta 越多。Delta gives the direction; gamma says how fast delta changes with the price. A position with positive delta and negative gamma is bullish, but each stretch TSLA rises makes its delta a little smaller: the further it rises (the further from the entry price), the less the next stretch earns. On the way down it is the reverse: delta grows as the price falls, and losses come faster and faster. With the position left unadjusted, the loss gamma causes depends only on how far TSLA has moved from the entry price in total, not on how many days it took (section 3 computes it). So it wants a slow rise: to get to the same place, the more days it takes, the more theta is collected day by day along the way.

gamma 和 vega 连起来读Reading gamma and vega together

同一到期日的仓位，gamma 和 vega 同号：买期权两者都为正，卖期权两者都为负。日历价差是例外：买 5/15 的 350 call、卖 4/24 的 350 call。同一个行权价，到期早的叫近月（这里是 4/24），到期晚的叫远月（5/15）。近月那张的 gamma 更大（0.0118 对 0.0082），远月那张的 vega 更大（0.41 对 0.24），相减之后 gamma −0.36、vega +16.98。它希望 TSLA 不大动，同时希望隐含波动率上涨。只看 gamma，会以为它和卖跨式赌的是同一件事；vega 的符号说明不是。For positions with a single expiration, gamma and vega have the same sign: both positive when buying options, both negative when selling. A calendar spread is the exception: buy the 5/15 350 call and sell the 4/24 350 call. At the same strike, the earlier expiration is called the front month (here 4/24), and the later one the back month (5/15). The front month has the larger gamma (0.0118 against 0.0082), the back month the larger vega (0.41 against 0.24), so after subtracting, gamma is −0.36 and vega +16.98. It wants TSLA not to move much, and at the same time wants implied volatility to rise. Looking only at gamma, you would think it bets on the same thing as a short straddle; the sign of vega shows it does not.

三估算一次变动的盈亏3Estimating the P&L of one move

四个数还能倒过来用：给定一次市场变动，估算仓位赚亏多少。先只用 delta，再把 gamma、theta、vega 加上。The four numbers can also be used the other way around: given a market move, estimate how much the position makes or loses. First with delta alone, then with gamma, theta and vega added.

一阶估算：只用 deltaFirst-order estimate: delta only

盈亏 ≈ Δ × ΔSP&L ≈ Δ × ΔS
delta × TSLA 变动了多少美元。假设这一路上 delta 一直不变delta × how many dollars TSLA moved, assuming delta stays the same the whole way

delta 是价值随股价变化的斜率。如果斜率一直不变，价值就沿一条直线变。可是 gamma 不为 0 时，TSLA 每动一点 delta 就跟着变，直线就不准了。Delta is the slope of value against the stock price. If the slope stayed constant, value would change along a straight line. But when gamma is not 0, delta changes with every little move in TSLA, and the straight line is no longer accurate.

二阶估算：讲义的写法Second-order estimate: the lecture notes' way

新 Δ ＝ 旧 Δ ＋ Γ × ΔSNew Δ = old Δ + Γ × ΔS
TSLA 动完之后的 delta：原来的 delta 加上 gamma 乘变动Delta after TSLA has moved: the original delta plus gamma times the move
盈亏 ≈ ΔS × (旧 Δ ＋ 新 Δ) ÷ 2P&L ≈ ΔS × (old Δ + new Δ) ÷ 2
一路上的 delta 用新旧两个值的平均来代表The average of the old and new values stands in for delta along the way

delta 在这一段变动里从旧值一路变到新值。价值的变化是这一路上每一小段「delta × 那一小段距离」加起来；delta 均匀变化时，就等于平均 delta 乘总距离。把新 Δ 代进去展开：Over this move delta goes all the way from the old value to the new one. The change in value is the sum, over each small step along the way, of "delta × that small distance"; when delta changes evenly, that equals the average delta times the total distance. Substitute the new Δ and expand:

ΔS × (Δ ＋ Δ ＋ Γ·ΔS) ÷ 2 ＝ Δ·ΔS ＋ ½·Γ·ΔS²ΔS × (Δ + Δ + Γ·ΔS) ÷ 2 = Δ·ΔS + ½·Γ·ΔS²
讲义的平均 delta 写法，和「一阶估算加一项 ½ × gamma × 变动的平方」是同一个式子The lecture notes' average-delta form is the same formula as "the first-order estimate plus a term ½ × gamma × move squared"

只用旧 delta，就漏掉了 ½·Γ·ΔS² 这一项。它和变动的平方成正比：TSLA 动 2 倍，这一项变成 4 倍；而且不管往哪边动，它的符号都跟着 gamma 走。gamma 为正的仓位，一阶估算总是偏低：大涨时少算了赚的，大跌时多算了亏的。gamma 为负的仓位反过来，一阶估算总是偏高。Using only the old delta misses the ½·Γ·ΔS² term. It is proportional to the square of the move: twice the TSLA move makes this term 4 times as large; and whichever way the price moves, its sign follows gamma. For a position with positive gamma, the first-order estimate is always too low: on a big rise it undercounts the gain, and on a big fall it overcounts the loss. For a position with negative gamma it is the reverse: the first-order estimate is always too high.

再把时间和隐含波动率的变化加上，就是估算一次变动的完整式子：Add the changes in time and implied volatility, and you have the full formula for estimating one move:

盈亏 ≈ Δ·ΔS ＋ ½·Γ·ΔS² ＋ Θ·Δt ＋ ν·ΔσP&L ≈ Δ·ΔS + ½·Γ·ΔS² + Θ·Δt + ν·Δσ
方向 ＋ 弯曲 ＋ 时间 ＋ 隐含波动率。Δt 是过了几天，Δσ 是隐含波动率变了几个点Direction + curvature + time + implied volatility. Δt is how many days passed; Δσ is how many points implied volatility changed
各条腿的隐含波动率变化不一样时，vega 那一项按腿分别算再相加When the legs' implied volatilities change by different amounts, compute the vega term leg by leg and add

四delta 截断陷阱4The delta clamping trap

用「新 Δ ＝ 旧 Δ ＋ Γ × ΔS」算新 delta 时，结果可能是一个不可能出现的值。讲义要求先截到边界，再求平均。When "new Δ = old Δ + Γ × ΔS" is used to compute the new delta, the result can be a value that is impossible. The lecture notes require clamping it to the bound first, then averaging.

每张期权的 delta 有硬边界：call 在 0 到 1 之间，put 在 −1 到 0 之间（讲义用整数格式写，就是 0 到 100、−100 到 0）。Every option's delta has hard bounds: a call between 0 and 1, a put between −1 and 0 (in the lecture notes' whole number format, 0 to 100 and −100 to 0).

  • call 深度实值时，到期几乎一定会行权，它就相当于一股股票：TSLA 涨 1 美元它涨 1 美元，不会涨得更多。A deep in-the-money call is almost certain to be exercised at expiration, so it is equivalent to one share of stock: when TSLA rises 1 dollar it rises 1 dollar, never more.
  • put 在 TSLA 大涨之后几乎一定作废，TSLA 再涨它也跌不下去，delta 最多回到 0。After a big TSLA rise a put is almost certain to expire worthless; if TSLA keeps rising it cannot fall any further, and its delta can at most return to 0.

线性外推不知道这两道边界。变动大，或者 gamma 大（临近到期的平值期权 gamma 最大，2.2 讲过），Γ × ΔS 就会把新 delta 推出边界。推出去的部分要截掉：call 截到 1、put 截到 0（下跌时 call 截到 0、put 截到 −1），再和旧 delta 求平均。Linear extrapolation does not know about these two bounds. With a large move, or a large gamma (at-the-money options close to expiration have the largest gamma, as 2.2 explained), Γ × ΔS pushes the new delta past the bound. The part pushed past has to be cut off: clamp a call to 1 and a put to 0 (on a fall, a call to 0 and a put to −1), then average with the old delta.

放到仓位里还有一层：整个仓位的 delta 没有边界，多买股票就能任意大。所以截断只能一条腿一条腿做，每条腿截完再相加，不能拿仓位合计去截。In a position there is one more layer: the whole position's delta has no bound, since buying more stock can make it as large as you like. So clamping can only be done leg by leg, clamping each leg and then adding; you cannot clamp the position total.

一阶估算First-order estimate

二阶估算Second-order estimate

假设 gamma 一路不变，用 旧 Δ ＋ Γ × ΔS 算新 delta。它不知道 delta 有边界。Assume gamma stays the same all the way and compute the new delta as old Δ + Γ × ΔS. It does not know delta has bounds.
delta 截断Delta clamping
