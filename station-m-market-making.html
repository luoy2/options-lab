做市报价Market making

节点 8 讲了波动率曲面：每个行权价、每个到期日各有一个隐含波动率。前面各节都站在买卖期权的人这一边，这一节换到对面：做市商怎样拿自己的理论值同时报出买价和卖价，边际从哪来，客户留给他的仓位怎么管。案例有两个：讲义里 March 95 call 的全过程，和 2026-04-13 这一天 TSLA 5/15 到期 350 call 的真实报价。Node 8 covered the volatility surface: every strike and every expiration has its own implied volatility. The earlier nodes all stood on the side of the people who buy and sell options. 〇谁在你对面0Who is on the other side

1.1 里买入 350 call，付的是卖价 21.10。这张 call 是谁卖出来的？In 1.1 you bought the 350 call and paid the ask, 21.10. Who sold you that call?

交易所里有两种人。一种是客户：个人投资者、基金经理，自己研究、自己决定什么时候买卖什么。另一种是做市商：他们不等行情，一直同时挂着一个买价和一个卖价，客户想买就卖给客户，客户想卖就从客户手里买。1.1 那笔 21.10 的买入，对面多半是做市商。An exchange has two kinds of participants. One is customers: individual investors and fund managers who do their own research and decide what to buy or sell, and when. The other is market makers: they do not wait for a view on the market. They always show a bid and an ask at the same time, sell to customers who want to buy and buy from customers who want to sell. The 21.10 purchase in 1.1 most likely had a market maker on the other side.

边际Edge

做市商心里有一个理论值：按自己认定的波动率算出来，这张期权值多少。边际量的是成交价离理论值有多远：A market maker has a theoretical value in mind: what the option is worth at the volatility it believes in. Edge measures how far the trade price is from the theoretical value:

这 9 美元只是算出来的数。卖出以后做市商手里多了一张空头 call，而接下来一个月 TSLA 涨了 20%（1.1）。讲义把做市归结成三个问题：That $9 is only a computed number. After the sale the market maker holds a short call, and over the next month TSLA rose 20% (1.1). The lecture notes boil market making down to three questions:

  1. 我认为这张期权值多少？What do I think this option is worth?
  2. 市场认为它值多少？What does the market think it is worth?
  3. 我手上已经背着什么仓位、什么风险？What position and what risk am I already carrying?

第一个问题给出边际，后两个问题决定这笔边际能不能留下来。本页后面几节按这三个问题展开。The first question gives the edge; the other two decide whether the edge can be kept. The rest of this page follows these three questions.

讲义说做市商常用波动率而不是价格来定报价的宽度。先看讲义的例子，再看 TSLA 整条期权链上的真实报价。The lecture notes say market makers often set the width of a quote in volatility rather than in price. First the example from the lecture notes, then real quotes across the whole TSLA option chain.

讲义的例子The lecture example

讲义 Session 10 给了一张估值表：标的是股票，离到期 57 天，利率 3%，波动率 19%。股价 100.50 时，有人来问 January 105 call 的市场。Lecture Session 10 gives a valuation table: the underlying is a stock, 57 days to expiration, interest rate 3%, volatility 19%. With the stock at 100.50, someone asks for a market in the January 105 call.

  1. 按 19% 算，这张 call 值 1.47，vega 0.141：波动率每变 1 个百分点，价格变 0.141。At 19%, this call is worth 1.47, with a vega of 0.141: for each percentage point of volatility, the price changes by 0.141.
  2. 买价按 18.5%、卖价按 19.5% 报，两边各差半个波动率点。换成价格，每边 0.141 × 0.5 ≈ 0.07。Quote the bid at 18.5% and the ask at 19.5%, half a volatility point on each side. In price, each side is 0.141 × 0.5 ≈ 0.07.
  3. 报出的市场：1.40 买，1.54 卖。The market quoted: 1.40 bid, 1.54 ask.
价格上的半个宽度 ＝ vega × 波动率上的半个宽度Half width in price = vega × half width in volatility
0.07 ≈ 0.141 × 0.5。直接用 18.5% 和 19.5% 定价是 1.402 和 1.543，结果一样0.07 ≈ 0.141 × 0.5. Pricing directly at 18.5% and 19.5% gives 1.402 and 1.543, the same result

讲义接着问：卖出 20 张之后怎么对冲？这张 call 的 delta 是 31.4，一张相当于 31.4 股，20 张空头相当于 −628 股。股票一手 100 股，买 600 股，剩下 −28 股对冲不上。往少的方向取：买 700 股会反过来多出 +72 股。The lecture notes go on: after selling 20 contracts, how do you hedge? The call's delta is 31.4, so one contract is equivalent to 31.4 shares and 20 short contracts to −628 shares. Stock trades in round lots of 100 shares: buy 600 shares and −28 shares stay unhedged. Rounding the other way, buying 700 shares leaves +72 shares in the opposite direction.

对冲去不掉的，是三节那张表里的 gamma 和 vega：实际波动变大、隐含波动率变动。模拟用的是真实中间价，隐含波动率的变化已经算在里面。4/13 这张 call 的隐含波动率（报价快照）从 9:31 的 47.9% 降到收盘 46.3%，少 1.6 个点；4/15 从 47.6% 升到 50.8%，盘中最高 52.7%，升了 3.3 个点，是第五节财报那次降幅（5.8 个点）的一半多。对冲以后仍然不亏，是因为库存小：每分钟按「库存 × vega × 隐含波动率的变化」估这一块，4/15 这天 vega 在 0.29 到 0.39 之间，200 次里最差一次偏移的约亏 3,000，不偏移的约亏 7,200，而一天的价差收入在 1.7 万以上。库存越大，这一块越大。去掉这部分风险，只能少接同方向的单子（偏移报价），或者买卖特征相似的期权（三节的最后一问）。模拟也没算买卖股票本身的成本。What hedging cannot remove is the gamma and vega in the table in section 3: realized volatility rising and implied volatility changing. The simulation uses real mids, so changes in implied volatility are already included. On 4/13 this call's implied volatility (quote snapshot) fell from 47.9% at 9:31 to 46.3% at the close, 1.6 points lower; on 4/15 it rose from 47.6% to 50.8%, with an intraday high of 52.7%, up 3.3 points, more than half of the drop after the earnings report in section 5 (5.8 points). The hedged runs still did not lose because inventory was small. Estimating this part each minute as inventory × vega × change in implied volatility, with vega between 0.29 and 0.39 on 4/15, the worst of 200 runs lost about 3,000 for the skewing market maker and about 7,200 for the one with no skew, while a day's spread income was above 17,000. The larger the inventory, the larger this part. The only ways to remove this risk are to take fewer orders in the same direction (skewed quotes) or to trade options with similar characteristics (the last question in section 3). The simulation also leaves out the cost of trading the stock itself.

五价差在补偿什么5What the spread pays for

做市商愿意报多窄的价，取决于这个价差能不能补上他背的风险。按讲义和上面的数据，至少有五笔。How narrow a market maker is willing to quote depends on whether the spread covers the risks it carries. From the lecture notes and the data above, there are at least five.

  1. 库存的价格风险。第四节：4/15 不偏移、不对冲的做市商，最差一天亏 101,570 美元，而一天的价差收入平均 18,609。Price risk on inventory. Section 4: on 4/15 the market maker that neither skews nor hedges lost $101,570 on its worst day, while a day's spread income averaged 18,609.

  2. 报价没来得及更新，知道得更多的人按旧价成交。这叫逆向选择。4/13 这张 350 call，如果有人每次都能早一分钟知道中间价，在 239 个一分钟里有 128 次（54%）能直接从当时的报价上赚钱：58 次在卖价买进、下一分钟中间价已经高过卖价，70 次反过来。平均每次每股 0.168，是这一天平均半个价差（0.067）的 2.5 倍。所以报价要跟着标的的每一次变动更新：晚更新一分钟，这一分钟里挂着的旧报价就会让做市商亏钱。Quotes not updated in time, and better-informed traders trading at the old price. This is called adverse selection. For the 350 call on 4/13, someone who always knew the mid one minute early could have made money straight off the quote at that moment in 128 of 239 minutes (54%): 58 times buying at the ask when the next minute's mid was already above the ask, and 70 times the other way round. The average gain was 0.168 per share each time, 2.5 times that day's average half spread (0.067). So quotes have to be updated with every move in the underlying: update one minute late, and the stale quote left up during that minute loses the market maker money.

A market maker cannot tell who knows more, so it quotes the same spread to everyone: the spread paid by uninformed customers has to cover what is lost to informed ones.

讲义的收尾How the lecture notes close

做市商Market maker
在交易所一直同时报出买价和卖价、谁来都跟他成交的交易商。A dealer on an exchange that always quotes a bid and an ask at the same time and trades with whoever comes.
客户、订单流Customer, order flow
自己决定买卖的一方；他们下单的先后与方向合起来叫订单流。The side that decides for itself what to buy and sell; the sequence and direction of their orders together are called order flow.
买价 bid、卖价 ask / offerBid, ask / offer
报价的人愿意买进、卖出的价格。客户卖只能卖到买价，买要付卖价。The prices at which the quoter is willing to buy and to sell. A customer selling can only get the bid; a customer buying has to pay the ask.
数量 sizeSize
每个价位愿意成交多少张，写成「买的张数 × 卖的张数」，例如 200 × 300。How many contracts are offered at each price, written as bid size × ask size, for example 200 × 300.
价差、中间价Spread, mid
价差 = 卖价 − 买价；中间价 = 两者的平均。Spread = ask − bid; mid = the average of the two.
理论值Theoretical value
按定价模型和自己认定的波动率算出的期权价值。The option value computed from a pricing model and the volatility you believe in.
边际 edgeEdge
成交价离理论值的距离 × 张数；按美元算再乘 100（一张 100 股），讲义的记法只乘张数（March 95 call 的 35.00）。成交时只是算出来的数，要靠对冲兑现或用转换锁定。The distance of the trade price from the theoretical value × contracts; in dollars, times 100 again (100 shares per contract). The lecture notes multiply only by contracts (the 35.00 on the March 95 call). At the trade it is only a computed number, realized through hedging or locked in with a conversion.
隐含波动率Implied volatility
把期权的市场价格代进定价模型，反推出来的波动率（1.2）。The volatility backed out by plugging an option's market price into a pricing model (1.2).
平滑隐含波动率Smoothed implied volatility
报价快照把各行权价的隐含波动率拟合成平滑曲线后，在某个行权价上的值。本页当作做市商的理论波动率。The value at one strike of the smooth curve the quote snapshot fits through the implied volatilities of all strikes. This page uses it as the market maker's theoretical volatility.
vega、波动率点Vega, volatility point
波动率每变 1 个百分点，期权价格变多少。价格宽度 = vega × 波动率宽度。How much the option price changes for each 1 percentage point change in volatility. Width in price = vega × width in volatility.
折成波动率点Width in vol points
按卖价反推的隐含波动率减去按买价反推的隐含波动率，约等于价差 ÷ vega。Implied volatility backed out from the ask minus implied volatility backed out from the bid, roughly spread ÷ vega.
delta、gamma、thetaDelta, gamma, theta
期权对标的的敏感度（2.1）、delta 变化的速度与时间价值的流失（2.2）。The option's sensitivity to the underlying (2.1), the speed at which delta changes, and the loss of time value (2.2).
平值、虚值、实值At, out of, in the money
行权价就在标的价附近的是平值；现在行权没有价值的是虚值（行权价高于标的的 call、低于标的的 put）；反过来是实值。At the money: the strike is right around the underlying price. Out of the money: exercising now has no value (a call with the strike above the underlying, a put with the strike below). The reverse is in the money.
远期价 FForward price F
标的按利率持有到到期日的价格（1.1）。4/13 的 TSLA 是 353.69。The price of holding the underlying at the interest rate until expiration (1.1). For TSLA on 4/13 it was 353.69.
Black-Scholes、Black 模型Black-Scholes, Black model
期权定价公式（2.1）；Black 模型是它用在期货期权上的版本，标的价换成期货价，期货型结算下不折现。The option pricing formula (2.1); the Black model is its version for futures options, with the underlying price replaced by the futures price and no discounting under futures-type settlement.
最小报价单位Minimum price increment
TSLA 期权 3 美元以上是 0.05，以下是 0.01（本页 25 天报价实测）。For TSLA options, 0.05 above $3 and 0.01 below (measured from 25 days of quotes on this page).
期货、期货期权Futures, futures option
期货是约定将来按某个价格买卖标的的合约，每天按结算价结清盈亏（2.3）；期货期权的标的是一张期货。A futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell the underlying at a set price in the future, with gains and losses settled daily at the settlement price (2.3); a futures option has a futures contract as its underlying.
期货型结算Futures-type settlement
期货期权不先付权利金，按日结算，定价不用折现（2.3）。The futures option's premium is not paid up front, it is settled daily, and pricing does not discount (2.3).
strangle、butterflyStrangle, butterfly
strangle：同一到期日一张虚值 put 加一张虚值 call。butterfly：行权价间距相等，买低的、卖两张中间的、买高的（3.2）。Strangle: one out-of-the-money put plus one out-of-the-money call with the same expiration. Butterfly: equally spaced strikes; buy the low one, sell two of the middle one, buy the high one (3.2).
pin risk
到期时期货收在行权价附近，不知道自己卖出的期权会不会被行权（5.2）。转换锁定利润之后只剩这一项。The futures settle near the strike at expiration and you do not know whether the option you sold will be exercised (5.2). Once a conversion locks in the profit, this is the only risk left.
C − P = F − X
期货期权的 put-call parity（5.2）。C、P：同一行权价的 call、put；F：期货价；X：行权价。Put-call parity for futures options (5.2). C, P: the call and put at the same strike; F: futures price; X: strike.
转换、反转换Conversion, reverse conversion
卖 call、买 put、买标的叫转换（conversion）；反过来叫反转换（5.2 叫 reversal）。两者都在建仓时锁定利润。Selling the call, buying the put and buying the underlying is a conversion; the reverse is a reverse conversion (called a reversal in 5.2). Both lock in the profit when the position is opened.
库存Inventory
做市商手上的净仓位，多头为正、空头为负。客户决定它往哪边走。The market maker's net position, positive when long and negative when short. Customers decide which way it moves.
按库存偏移Skew by inventory
多头时整体压低报价、空头时整体抬高，让库存往 0 回；代价是少挣一部分价差。Lower the whole quote when long and raise it when short, so inventory moves back toward 0; the cost is earning less of the spread.
逆向选择Adverse selection
和你成交的人比你知道得多，他们成交之后价格多半往对你不利的方向走。The people trading with you know more than you do, and after they trade the price usually moves against you.
