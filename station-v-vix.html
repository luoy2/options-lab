〇为什么要直接交易波动率0Why trade volatility directly

4.2 讲过：买一张期权，用标的做动态对冲，盈亏大体看实际波动和隐含波动差多少，但还要看大涨大跌落在哪几天。4.2 那 24 天按日历折算的实际波动 47.4%，比买入时的隐含波动 46.3% 还高，账本却亏了 34.53，因为对冲的收入只在 gamma 大的日子进账。讲义 S10 接着问：这条路靠得住吗？As 4.2 showed, if you buy an option and hedge it dynamically with the underlying, the profit or loss depends broadly on the gap between realized and implied volatility, but also on which days the big moves land. Over the 24 days in 4.2, realized volatility on a calendar-day basis was 47.4%, above the 46.3% implied at purchase, yet the book lost 34.53, because hedging income only comes in on days when gamma is large. Lecture S10 then asks: is this route reliable?

Each day's squared move is that day's variance, and a month's variance is the sum of the daily ones. A variance contract adds up the squares of the daily moves directly, so a big move counts the same whichever day it lands on, unlike the hedging book in 4.2, which depends on whether the option's gamma was large that day. Section 2 will also show that what a row of options plus daily hedging replicates is exactly variance.

二一排期权，每个行权价买多少2A row of options: how many at each strike

讲义把 VIX 的设计写成两个问题：怎样用期权搭一个 vega 头寸，让它的价值等于一份波动率合约？怎样让这个头寸的 vega 不随行情变，到期时两者对得上？The lecture frames the design of VIX as two questions: how do you build a vega position out of options whose value equals a volatility contract? And how do you keep that position's vega from changing as the market moves, so that the two match at expiration?

2.3 讲过 vega：隐含波动率变 1 个点，期权价格变多少。一份波动率合约对波动率的敞口是固定的；要用期权复制它，期权头寸的 vega 也得固定，不能随标的价格变。讲义分三步走到答案。2.3 covered vega: how much an option's price changes when implied volatility changes by 1 point. A volatility contract has a fixed exposure to volatility; to replicate it with options, the options position's vega must also be fixed and must not change with the underlying price. The lecture reaches the answer in three steps.

第一步：一张期权不够Step 1: one option is not enough

这一步起要用三个词。行权价正好在标的现价附近的期权叫平值。call 的行权价高于现价、put 的行权价低于现价，叫虚值：现在行权拿不到钱，价格全是时间价值。反过来，call 的行权价低于现价、put 的行权价高于现价，叫实值：现在行权就能拿到钱，这部分叫内在价值。离现价越远，就叫深实值、深虚值。From this step on, three terms are needed. An option whose strike is right around the current price of the underlying is at the money. A call with its strike above the current price, or a put with its strike below it, is out of the money: exercising now pays nothing, and its price is all time value. Conversely, a call with its strike below the current price, or a put with its strike above it, is in the money: exercising now pays something, and that part is called intrinsic value. The further from the current price, the deeper in or out of the money.

一张期权的 vega 在标的正好在行权价附近时最大，标的往哪边走远，vega 都变小。TSLA 在 350 时，350 的期权 vega 最大；TSLA 涨到 450，它成了深实值，vega 所剩无几。想让 vega 一直都在，手里就得一直有一张平值期权。标的会走到哪里事先不知道，所以每个行权价都要买。An option's vega is largest when the underlying is right around its strike, and shrinks as the underlying moves away in either direction. With TSLA at 350, the 350 options have the largest vega; if TSLA rises to 450, they are deep in the money and little vega is left. To keep vega there all the time, you must always hold an at-the-money option. You do not know in advance where the underlying will go, so you have to buy every strike.

第二步：每个行权价各买一张，还是不平Step 2: one option at each strike is still not flat

讲义说，这样仍然得不到恒定的 vega：行权价高的平值期权，vega 比行权价低的平值期权大。原因有两个，两个都和价格成正比：The lecture says this still does not give a constant vega: an at-the-money option at a high strike has more vega than an at-the-money option at a low strike. There are two reasons, both proportional to price:

  • 平值期权的 vega 和价格成正比。σ ＝ 46.3%、30 天，标的 200 时 200 的平值期权 vega 是 0.228，标的 400 时 400 的平值期权是 0.456。价格翻倍，vega 翻倍。An at-the-money option's vega is proportional to price. With σ = 46.3% and 30 days, the at-the-money 200 option with the underlying at 200 has vega 0.228; the at-the-money 400 option with the underlying at 400 has 0.456. Double the price, double the vega.
  • 同时起作用的行权价，档数也和价格成正比。行权价按美元等距排（下图每 10 美元一档），而一个标准差的美元宽度和价格成正比：价格 200 时约 26.5 美元，400 时约 53.1 美元。落在标的 ±1 个标准差以内的行权价，200 时约 5 档，400 时约 11 档。The number of strikes in play at once is also proportional to price. Strikes are spaced evenly in dollars (every $10 in the chart below), while one standard deviation in dollars is proportional to price: about $26.5 at a price of 200 and about $53.1 at 400. About 5 strikes lie within ±1 standard deviation of the underlying at 200, and about 11 at 400.

两个因素相乘，每档一张时的总 vega 按价格的平方变：以标的 350 时为 1，标的 250 时是 0.51，450 时是 1.65。Multiply the two factors and the total vega with one option per strike scales with the square of the price: taking the underlying at 350 as 1, it is 0.51 at 250 and 1.65 at 450.

第三步：按 1/K² 配Step 3: weight by 1/K²

要抵掉价格的平方，每个行权价买的数量就按行权价平方的倒数配：低行权价多买，高行权价少买。以 350 买 1 张为准，175 买 (350 ÷ 175)² ＝ 4 张，700 买 0.25 张。这样总 vega 在标的 250、350、450 时是同一个数。讲义公式页顶上单独写着的 1/strike²，就是这个配方。To cancel the square of the price, the quantity at each strike is weighted by the inverse square of the strike: more at low strikes, fewer at high strikes. Taking 1 option at 350 as the base, buy (350 ÷ 175)² = 4 at 175 and 0.25 at 700. Then the total vega is the same number with the underlying at 250, 350 or 450. Under Black-Scholes, gamma times the square of the stock price is proportional to vega, so it does not change either: in the same model, one option per strike gives 0.51 : 1 : 1.65, and weighting by 1/K² gives 1.00 : 1 : 1.00.

光买下这排期权还不是方差合约：它有 delta，标的一动，它的价值就跟着变。还要像 4.2 那样每天用标的把 delta 对冲掉。4.2 的账本缺的正是「gamma 乘股价的平方每天一样」这个条件，在这排期权上它自动成立，于是对冲下来的盈亏只看每天涨跌的平方加起来，比买入时的隐含方差多还是少，这就复制了一份方差合约。这一步是推导的结论，讲义给了配方，没有给证明。VIX 就是按这个配方，给一整排真实期权算出来的价值。Just buying this row of options is not yet a variance contract: it has delta, so its value moves as soon as the underlying moves. You also have to hedge the delta with the underlying every day, as in 4.2. What the 4.2 book lacked was exactly the condition that gamma times the square of the stock price is the same every day. On this row of options it holds automatically, so the hedged profit or loss depends only on whether the sum of squared daily moves is more or less than the implied variance at purchase, and that replicates a variance contract. This step is a result of derivation; the lecture gives the recipe but not the proof. VIX is the value of a whole row of real options computed with this recipe.

为什么乘 2 ÷ T：按 1/K² 配好的这一排虚值期权，如果每个行权价的隐含波动率都是同一个 σ，它们的价值 Σ(ΔK/K²)·erT·Q 加起来约等于 σ² × T ÷ 2，也就是到期前总方差的一半。乘 2 还原成到期前的总方差，再除以 T 换成每年的方差。拿第四节 TSLA 5/15 这一串验证：每档都按平值的 46.07% 定价，Σ 只比 σ² × T ÷ 2 多 0.8%，代进公式算回 46.11%。为什么正好是一半，是推导的结论，讲义没有给证明。

Why multiply by 2 ÷ T: if every strike had the same implied volatility σ, the value of this row of out-of-the-money options weighted by 1/K², Σ(ΔK/K²)·erT·Q, would add up to about σ² × T ÷ 2, half the total variance to expiration. Multiplying by 2 restores the total variance to expiration, and dividing by T turns it into variance per year. Checking with the TSLA 5/15 chain from section 4: with every strike priced at the at-the-money 46.07%, Σ is only 0.8% above σ² × T ÷ 2, and plugging it into the formula gives back 46.11%. Why it is exactly half is a result of derivation; the lecture gives no proof.

七VIX 期货与期权7VIX futures and options

VIX 本身买不到。以它结算的期货和期权，是隐含波动率合约。VIX itself cannot be bought. The futures and options that settle on it are implied volatility contracts.

VIX 期货怎么结算How VIX futures settle

期货是今天约定一个价，到期按结算值和约定价的差额收付现金，和 1.1 的远期合约是同一件事。不同的是期货在交易所买卖，每天按当天的期货价格结一次账：价格涨了，买方当天就收到涨的那部分，卖方付出，而不是等到期一次算清。A futures contract agrees on a price today and at expiration pays cash equal to the difference between the settlement value and the agreed price, the same thing as the forward contract in 1.1. The difference is that futures trade on an exchange and are settled each day at that day's futures price: if the price rises, the buyer receives the rise that same day and the seller pays it, instead of everything being settled at once at expiration.

讲义说，VIX 期货在 SPX 期权到期日之前 30 天的那个周三结算，结算值是那天的 VIX。选这一天的原因讲义没写：那天离下一个 SPX 期权到期日正好 30 天，VIX 只用这一个到期日就能算出来，不用插值。实际结算用的不是那天收盘的 VIX，而是当天早上 Cboe 用 SPX 期权的开盘价专门算的一次 VIX（叫特别开盘报价，SOQ）。到期之前，期货价格是市场对结算那天 VIX 的估计，不等于今天的 VIX。The lecture says VIX futures settle on the Wednesday 30 days before an SPX option expiration, and the settlement value is that day's VIX. The lecture does not say why that day: it is exactly 30 days before the next SPX option expiration, so VIX can be computed from that one expiration alone, without interpolation. The actual settlement does not use that day's closing VIX but a special VIX that Cboe computes that morning from the opening prices of SPX options (called the Special Opening Quotation, SOQ). Before expiration, the futures price is the market's estimate of VIX on settlement day, not today's VIX.

讲义接着说：股票波动率指数的期限结构有时是 backward，近月高于远月；更常见的是 contango，远月高于近月。在 contango 下，随着时间过去，一张期货离到期越来越近，它的价格往下滑向近月的水平，价值必然下降。所以讲义说，买入 VIX 期货要赚钱，至少要发生下面一件事：The lecture goes on: the term structure of a stock volatility index is sometimes backward, with the near month above the far months; more often it is in contango, with far months above the near month. Under contango, as time passes a futures contract gets closer to expiration and its price slides down toward the near-month level, so its value has to fall. So the lecture says that for a long VIX futures position to make money, at least one of the following has to happen:

  1. 整条 contango 曲线往上移；the whole contango curve shifts up;
  2. 期限结构从 contango 翻成 backward；the term structure flips from contango to backward;

VIX 期权VIX options

VIX 期权是欧式的（只能在到期那天行权，节点 9 讲过美式与欧式的区别），现金结算：到期也在那个周三，和同月的 VIX 期货按同一个 VIX 值结算。所以它实际上是那张同月 VIX 期货的期权。VIX options are European (they can be exercised only on expiration day; node 9 covered American versus European) and cash-settled: they also expire on that Wednesday and settle on the same VIX value as the VIX futures of the same month. So in effect they are options on that month's VIX futures.

讲义：想兑现一张期权的波动率价值，就要动态对冲。VIX 本身买不到，VIX 期权只能用 VIX 期货对冲，所以决定 VIX 期权价值的，是 VIX 期货的波动率，不是 VIX 指数的波动率。讲义给了 2015-05-21 的历史波动率：The lecture: to cash in on an option's volatility value, you have to hedge dynamically. VIX itself cannot be bought and VIX options can only be hedged with VIX futures, so what drives the value of VIX options is the volatility of VIX futures, not of the VIX index. VIX 指数自己的年化波动率超过 100%。越往后的月份越不波动：20 日那一列，从指数到第 1 个月乘 0.62，再往后每个月乘 0.58、0.69，第 3 个月只有指数的四分之一（116.65 对 28.91，4.03 倍）。这是均值回复落在期货上的样子：远月期货的结算日还远，今天的冲高到那时多半已经回落，所以它不跟着今天冲。用指数的波动率给远月的 VIX 期权定价，会高估好几倍。The VIX index's own annualized volatility is over 100%. This is what mean reversion looks like in futures: a far-month future's settlement day is still far away, and today's spike will probably have faded by then, so it does not follow today's spike. covered call 是空波动率A covered call is short volatility

讲义最后一个例子：组合经理有两个目标，一是让组合增值，二是跑赢市场。他持有一篮子股票，同时卖出这些股票的 call 收权利金，这叫 covered call。什么情况下两个目标能同时达到？The lecture's last example: a portfolio manager has two goals, to grow the portfolio and to beat the market. They hold a basket of stocks and also sell calls on those stocks to collect premium; this is called a covered call. When can both goals be met at once?

两个目标只在平静的市场里同时达到，所以 covered call 是一个空波动率的头寸，市场越不波动越好。讲义说，这个空波动率头寸可以用波动率合约来对冲：买入一份波动率合约，市场大动时它赚钱，抵掉 covered call 在大涨大跌时吃的亏。Both goals are met only in a calm market, so a covered call is a short volatility position: the less the market moves, the better. The lecture says this short volatility position can be hedged with a volatility contract: buy a volatility contract, which makes money when the market moves a lot and offsets what the covered call loses on big rises and drops.
