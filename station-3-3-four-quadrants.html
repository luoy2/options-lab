四象限与选择Four quadrants and choosing

3.2 用 2026-04-13 收盘 TSLA 5/15 到期的真实报价，搭出了教材里的全部结构，按到期盈亏看它们押的是大动还是横盘。这一节把每个结构压成 gamma 和 vega 两个数，按正负号分进四个格子；再从一个市场观点出发，一步步定到该用哪一族结构。3.2 used real TSLA quotes for the 5/15 expiration at the 2026-04-13 close to build every structure in the textbook, and read their P&L at expiration to see whether each one bets on a big move or on a flat market. 〇两个问题，四个格子0Two questions, four quadrants

不押方向的仓位，赌的是两件事：标的实际会动多少，隐含波动率会涨还是会跌。A position that does not bet on direction is betting on two things: how much the underlying will actually move, and whether implied volatility will rise or fall.

3.2 的结构大多 delta 接近 0，不押方向（2.4：delta 是整个仓位相当于多少股）。剩下的赌注可以拆成两个问题，各由一个希腊字母回答：Most structures in 3.2 have delta close to 0 and do not bet on direction (2.4: delta is how many shares the whole position is equivalent to). The remaining bet splits into two questions, each answered by one Greek:

  • 标的实际会动多少。由 Γ 回答。2.2 讲过，标的动了 ΔS，gamma 带来的那一块是 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²：gamma 为正时涨跌都多赚，动得越多赚得越多；为负时涨跌都多亏。How much the underlying will actually move. Answered by Γ. As 2.2 showed, when the underlying moves ΔS, the part that comes from gamma is ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²: with positive gamma you make extra on both rises and falls, and the bigger the move the more you make; with negative gamma you lose extra on both.
  • 隐含波动率会涨还是跌。由 ν 回答。vega 为正，隐含波动率涨就赚；为负，跌就赚（2.3）。Whether implied volatility will rise or fall. Answered by ν. With positive vega you make money when implied volatility rises; with negative vega, when it falls (2.3).

theta 不用单独问。2.2 讲过模型里 Θ ≈ −½σ²S²Γ（这是一年的量，除以 365 是每天）：不计利息那一项时，theta 的正负号和 gamma 相反。第二节的表里，每个结构都核对了这一条，全部成立。Theta needs no separate question. As 2.2 showed, in the model Θ ≈ −½σ²S²Γ (a yearly amount; divide by 365 for one day): leaving out the interest term, theta always has the opposite sign of gamma. Every structure in the table in section 2 was checked against this, and it holds for all of them.

一同一到期日，gamma 和 vega 同号1One expiration: gamma and vega have the same sign

vega 可以从 gamma 算出来。两者量的是同一样东西：期权价值随标的价格弯了多少。Vega can be computed from gamma. Both measure the same thing: how much the option's value curves as the underlying price changes.

分布变宽，为什么期权会变贵Why options get more expensive when the distribution widens

隐含波动率涨 1 个点，意思是市场认为到期时价格会散得更开（1.2：σ 是分布的宽度）。期权的价值是所有可能结果按概率的平均（1.1 定价四步）。散得更开，对这个平均有什么影响，要看价值随价格是直的还是弯的：Implied volatility rising 1 point means the market expects prices to be more spread out at expiration (1.2: σ is the width of the distribution). An option's value is the probability-weighted average of all possible outcomes (1.1, the four pricing steps). What a wider spread does to that average depends on whether value is straight or curved in price:

  • 如果价值随价格是一条直线（一股股票就是这样），价格往上多走一段多赚的，和往下多走一段多亏的一样多，平均起来不变。散得再开也不值钱。If value is a straight line in price (as it is for a share of stock), the extra gain from the price going further up equals the extra loss from it going further down, and the average does not change. A wider spread is worth nothing.
  • 如果价值向上弯（gamma 为正），往上多走一段多赚的，比往下多走一段多亏的多；往下走到很远时甚至不再亏。散得越开，平均越高。If value curves upward (positive gamma), the extra gain from going further up is larger than the extra loss from going further down; far enough down, it stops losing at all. The wider the spread, the higher the average.

2.2 算过弯曲带来的那一块：标的动了 ΔS，多出 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²。对所有可能的 ΔS 取平均，(ΔS)² 的平均就是价格的方差，也就是一个标准差的平方。1.2 讲过一个标准差是 F × σ × √t；这里用现价 S 近似 F（352.36 对 353.69），一个标准差是 S × σ × √t，平方是 S² × σ² × t。σ 涨 1 个点（0.01），方差多出 S² × t × [(σ + 0.01)² − σ²]，约等于 S² × t × 2σ × 0.01。期权价值多出一半 gamma 乘以这个数：2.2 computed the part that comes from curvature: when the underlying moves ΔS, it adds ½ × Γ × (ΔS)². Averaged over all possible ΔS, the average of (ΔS)² is the price's variance, the square of one standard deviation. 1.2 showed that one standard deviation is F × σ × √t; here the current price S stands in for F (352.36 against 353.69), so one standard deviation is S × σ × √t and its square is S² × σ² × t. When σ rises 1 point (0.01), the variance grows by S² × t × [(σ + 0.01)² − σ²], about S² × t × 2σ × 0.01. The option's value grows by half of gamma times this number:

ν ≈ Γ × S² × σ × t ÷ 100ν ≈ Γ × S² × σ × t ÷ 100
vega ≈ gamma × 股价的平方 × 隐含波动率 × 离到期年数，再除以 100（vega 按 1 个点算，1 个点是 0.01）vega ≈ gamma × the stock price squared × implied volatility × years to expiration, divided by 100 (vega is per 1 point, and 1 point is 0.01)
在 Black-Scholes 模型里这个等式是精确的；报价快照里的数出自另一套模型，下表里差 3% 以内In the Black-Scholes model this equation is exact; the numbers in the quote snapshot come from a different model and differ by less than 3% in the table below

加起来还是同号Added up, still the same sign

同一个到期日里，每条腿的 S² 和 t 都一样，只有 σ 不同。5/15 这一串的 σ 在 45% 到 51% 之间，所以每条腿的 vega 都是它 gamma 的 49 到 57 倍。把几条腿加起来：Within one expiration, every leg has the same S² and t; only σ differs. Across this 5/15 chain σ is between 45% and 51%, so every leg's vega is 49 to 57 times its gamma. Add several legs together:

净 ν ≈ 净 Γ × 约 50net ν ≈ net Γ × about 50
同一到期日的仓位，净 vega 大约是净 gamma 的 50 倍（TSLA 4/13、5/15 到期），两者正负号相同For a position with one expiration, net vega is about 50 times net gamma (TSLA 4/13, 5/15 expiration), and the two have the same sign

所以只用一个到期日，不管怎么搭，gamma 和 vega 的正负号基本都相同，只能落在右上或左下。要落到左上、右下，得用两个到期日：t 不同，倍数就不同（第四节）。So with only one expiration, however you build the position, gamma and vega nearly always have the same sign, and it can only land in the top-right or bottom-left quadrant. To land in the top left or bottom right you need two expirations: a different t gives a different multiple (section 4).

这条规则是近似的。几条腿的 gamma 大半互相抵掉时，净 vega 的正负号可能由各条腿倍数的差别决定，和净 gamma 不同号。建仓这天 5/15 各行权价的倍数在 49 到 57 之间，差得不多。第三节的逐日回放里，后来这几个倍数差开了，同一只 butterfly 有 6 天翻了过去。This rule is approximate. When most of the legs' gamma cancels out, the sign of net vega can be decided by the differences between the legs' multiples, and differ from the sign of net gamma. On the entry day the multiples for the 5/15 strikes were between 49 and 57, not far apart. In the day-by-day replay in section 3 these multiples later spread apart, and the same butterfly flipped on 6 days.

三butterfly 为什么和 strangle 反号3Why the butterfly and the strangle have opposite signs

long strangle 是正 gamma，long butterfly 却是负 gamma，两个都要付钱买。从每条腿的 gamma 加起来看。A long strangle has positive gamma, but a long butterfly has negative gamma, and you pay to buy either one. Look at it by adding up each leg's gamma.

2.2 讲过：每张买入的期权 gamma 为正，在自己的行权价附近最大，离得越远越小，画出来像一座小山。卖出一张，曲线上下翻过来，处处为负。一个结构的 gamma，是各条腿的曲线在同一个价位上加起来。As 2.2 showed, every option you buy has positive gamma, largest near its own strike and smaller the farther away, drawn as a small hill. Sell one and the curve flips upside down, negative everywhere. A structure's gamma is its legs' curves added up at the same price.

「买入 butterfly」的「买入」说的是付钱、买两翼，不是 gamma 的方向。数总张数也不管用：+1、−2、+1 加起来是 0。要按张数比较标的附近各条腿的 gamma：建仓时身离标的最近，又是两张，卖出的 gamma 比买入的多。这和 3.1 的「平值腿在买方还是卖方」是同一条规则。condor 也一样：卖出的 340、360 离 352.36 最近，净 gamma −0.174。The "long" in "long butterfly" refers to paying money and buying the wings, not to the direction of gamma. Counting total contracts does not help either: +1, −2, +1 adds up to 0. Compare the gamma of the legs near the underlying, weighted by contracts: at entry the body is closest to the underlying and has two contracts, so the gamma sold exceeds the gamma bought. This is the same rule as 3.1's "is the at-the-money leg bought or sold". The condor works the same way: the sold 340 and 360 are closest to 352.36, and net gamma is −0.174.

At the same time, with little time left, a 1-point rise in implied volatility cannot widen the distribution at expiration by much, so vega is small. In Black-Scholes, an at-the-money option's gamma is roughly inversely proportional to √t, and its vega roughly proportional to √t.

为什么拿实际和隐含比Why compare actual with implied

正 gamma 的仓位每天付 theta，每次标的动了收 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²。theta 付多少，由隐含波动率定：Θ ≈ −½σ²S²Γ 里的 σ 就是隐含波动率。收多少，由之后真实动了多少定。所以实际波动率高于隐含，收的比付的多，正 gamma 划算；低于隐含，负 gamma 划算。4.2 把这条线叫盈亏平衡波动率，它就等于隐含波动率。A positive-gamma position pays theta every day and collects ½ × Γ × (ΔS)² every time the underlying moves. How much theta it pays is set by implied volatility: the σ in Θ ≈ −½σ²S²Γ is implied volatility. How much it collects is set by how much the underlying really moves afterward. So when actual volatility is above implied, it collects more than it pays and positive gamma comes out ahead; below implied, negative gamma does. 4.2 calls this line the breakeven volatility, and it equals implied volatility.

TSLA 4/13：350 call 的隐含波动率是 46.3%，入场前一年的历史波动率是 49.3%（1.2）。拿历史来预测，会觉得实际波动会高于隐含，该选右上格。事后量出这 24 天的实际波动率是 45.8%（1.2），和 46.3% 几乎一样：按波动率算，买方和卖方谁都没占到便宜。历史波动率只是做预测的材料，本身不是之后会发生的事。TSLA on 4/13: the 350 call's implied volatility was 46.3%, and historical volatility over the year before entry was 49.3% (1.2). Using history as the forecast, you would expect actual volatility to beat implied and pick the top-right quadrant. Measured afterward, actual volatility over these 24 days was 45.8% (1.2), almost the same as 46.3%: in volatility terms, neither the buyer nor the seller came out ahead. Historical volatility is only material for a forecast; it is not itself what will happen next.

vega 那两行为什么要 calendarWhy the vega rows need a calendar

同一到期日的结构，gamma 和 vega 同号，等于同时押两件事：实际会动多少、隐含波动率往哪边走，而且押的方向一样。两个看法一致时，这没有问题。看法相反时，就要 calendar 把两件事拆开。A structure with one expiration has gamma and vega of the same sign, so it bets on two things at once, how much the underlying will actually move and which way implied volatility will go, and it bets the same way on both. When your two views agree, that is fine. When they point opposite ways, you need a calendar to separate the two.

买入 calendar 是负 gamma、正 vega：标的实际动得多，它亏；隐含波动率涨，它赚。讲义说隐含波动率要「涨得至少和实际波动率一样快」，意思是隐含涨带来的钱，要够补标的动起来造成的亏损。卖出 calendar 反过来。A long calendar has negative gamma and positive vega: it loses when the underlying actually moves a lot, and makes money when implied volatility rises. When the lecture notes say implied volatility must "rise at least as fast as actual volatility", they mean the money from implied volatility rising has to cover the loss from the underlying's movement. A short calendar is the reverse.

隐含波动率确实会自己动。4/22 收盘后 TSLA 发财报，第二天 350 call 的隐含波动率从 48.55% 降到 43.16%（2.3、3.1 讲过这一天）。对所有 vega 为正的仓位，这一跌都是亏损的一项；标的那天动得够多，gamma 那一项才可能补回来。Implied volatility does move on its own. TSLA reported earnings after the close on 4/22, and the next day the 350 call's implied volatility fell from 48.55% to 43.16% (2.3 and 3.1 covered this day). For every position with positive vega, that drop is a loss; only if the underlying moved enough that day could the gamma part make it back.

六从观点到结构6From view to structure

一个市场观点，按四个问题问下去：方向、实际会动多少、隐含波动率、要几个到期日。问完就落到一格，再从格里挑结构。Take a market view through four questions in turn: direction, how much the underlying will actually move, implied volatility, and how many expirations are needed. At the end it lands in one quadrant, and you pick a structure from that quadrant.

  1. 方向。看涨要 delta 为正，看跌为负，不知道就接近 0。3.1 讲过带方向的两种价差：看涨用牛市价差，看跌用熊市价差。Direction. Bullish wants positive delta, bearish negative, and no view wants delta close to 0. 3.1 covered the two spreads with a direction: a bull spread for a bullish view and a bear spread for a bearish one.
  2. 实际会动多少，和隐含比。比隐含多，gamma 为正；比隐含少，为负。How much it will actually move, compared with implied. More than implied, positive gamma; less than implied, negative.
  3. 隐含波动率会涨还是跌。涨，vega 为正；跌，为负。Whether implied volatility will rise or fall. Rise, positive vega; fall, negative.
  4. gamma 和 vega 同号吗。同号，一个到期日就够；反号，要两个到期日。Do gamma and vega have the same sign? Same sign, one expiration is enough; opposite signs, two expirations are needed.

第 2、3 问有时只答得出一个。同一到期日时，另一个会跟着同号，要检查它是不是你能接受的。Sometimes you can answer only one of questions 2 and 3. With one expiration, the other follows with the same sign; check whether that is something you can accept.

      买卖张数不相等的结构。净买入期权落在右上，净卖出落在左下（3.2）。A structure with unequal numbers bought and sold. Net bought options land in the top right, net sold in the bottom left (3.2).
      牛市价差 / 熊市价差Bull spread / bear spread
      同一到期日、两个行权价的一对期权。牛市价差买低卖高，delta 为正；熊市价差买高卖低，delta 为负（3.1）。A pair of options with one expiration and two strikes. A bull spread buys the lower strike and sells the higher, with positive delta; a bear spread buys the higher and sells the lower, with negative delta (3.1).
      calendar：近月与远月Calendar: front month and back month
      同一行权价、两个到期日。到期早的叫近月，晚的叫远月。买入 calendar 是买远月、卖近月（3.2）。Same strike, two expirations. The earlier one is the front month, the later one the back month. A long calendar buys the back month and sells the front month (3.2).
