〇理论值怎么变成现金0How theoretical value becomes cash

讲义开头说，理论定价模型要做三件事：算出理论值；量化风险；给出一个办法，把期权的价值和市场价格之间的差额真正拿到手。前两件是 1.1 到 2.4 的内容，这一节讲第三件。The lecture notes open by saying a theoretical pricing model has three jobs: compute a theoretical value; quantify the risk; and give a method for actually capturing the difference between the option's value and its market price. The first two are the content of 1.1 through 2.4. This node covers the third.

按 42.56% 的波动率用 Black-Scholes 算，这张 call 值 6.55（不四舍五入是 6.5496），市场只卖 5.50。With Black-Scholes at 42.56% volatility, this call is worth 6.55 (6.5496 before rounding). The market sells it for only 5.50.

讲义的钱都按「张 × 点」记：张数乘每股的价格差。1 张合约对应 100 股，换成美元要再乘 100。买 100 张，按理论值算多赚 100 × (6.55 − 5.50) ＝ 105.00，换成美元是 10,500。The lecture notes count money in "contracts × points": number of contracts times the price difference per share. One contract covers 100 shares, so converting to dollars means multiplying by another 100. Buying 100 contracts earns, at theoretical value, an extra 100 × (6.55 − 5.50) = 105.00, which is $10,500.

问题是这 105 怎么拿到手。到期那天，call 只值它的内在价值 max(标的价 − 100, 0)，和理论值没有关系：标的收在 100 以下，100 张 call 一文不值，付出的 550（55,000 美元）全亏，买得再便宜也没用。只买期权、什么都不做，赌的是到期那天标的在哪。The question is how to actually collect this 105. On expiration day the call is worth only its intrinsic value max(underlying price − 100, 0), which has nothing to do with theoretical value. If the underlying closes below 100, the 100 calls are worthless and the 550 paid ($55,000) is lost, no matter how cheaply you bought them. Buying the option and doing nothing is a bet on where the underlying ends up on expiration day.

第一步：delta 中性Step one: delta neutral

2.1 讲过，delta 的一种读法是对冲比率。这张 call 的 delta 是 50（讲义用整数写法，50 就是 0.50）：标的涨 1，call 涨约 0.50。讲义里 1 张标的合约和 1 张期权对应的股数一样，都是 100 股，所以 1 张标的的 delta 记作 100。100 张 call 相当于 50 张标的。同时卖空 50 张标的，两边对标的小涨小跌的反应正好抵消：2.1 explained that one way to read delta is as a hedge ratio. This call's delta is 50 (the lecture notes write deltas as whole numbers; 50 means 0.50): when the underlying rises 1, the call rises about 0.50. In the lecture notes one underlying contract and one option contract cover the same 100 shares, so one underlying contract has a delta of 100. 100 calls are equivalent to 50 underlying contracts. Sell short 50 underlying contracts at the same time, and the two sides' responses to small moves in the underlying cancel exactly:

仓位 Δ ＝ 100 × 50 − 50 × 100 ＝ 0Position Δ = 100 × 50 − 50 × 100 = 0
100 张 call 每张 50 delta，减去卖出的 50 张标的每张 100 delta100 calls at 50 delta each, minus 50 underlying contracts sold at 100 delta each

整个仓位的 delta 为 0，叫delta 中性。这时仓位不再赌方向。The whole position has a delta of 0. This is called delta neutral. The position no longer bets on direction.

第二步：调仓Step two: adjustments

中性只维持一瞬间。2.2 讲过，标的一动，call 的 delta 自己会变。一周后标的涨到 99.15，delta 变成 54：Neutrality lasts only a moment. As 2.2 explained, when the underlying moves, the call's delta changes by itself. A week later the underlying is up to 99.15 and delta is 54:

仓位 Δ ＝ 100 × 54 − 50 × 100 ＝ +400Position Δ = 100 × 54 − 50 × 100 = +400
call 这边多出 400 delta，仓位又开始赌上涨The call side now has 400 extra delta, and the position is betting on a rise again

卖出 4 张标的，把 +400 拉回 0。讲义把这样的一笔交易叫调仓。再过一周标的跌到 92.35，delta 降到 36：Sell 4 underlying contracts to bring +400 back to 0. The lecture notes call such a trade an adjustment. One more week later the underlying falls to 92.35 and delta drops to 36:

仓位 Δ ＝ 100 × 36 − 50 × 100 − 400 ＝ −1,800Position Δ = 100 × 36 − 50 × 100 − 400 = −1,800
最后那个 −400 是第一周卖出的 4 张。算仓位 delta 时，以前所有的调仓都要算进去The final −400 is the 4 contracts sold in week one. Position delta has to include every earlier adjustment

买回 18 张。每隔一段时间看一次新的 delta、调一次仓，一直做到到期，叫动态对冲。Buy back 18 contracts. Checking the new delta at regular intervals and adjusting each time, all the way to expiration, is called dynamic hedging.

讲义接着问：把整个期限里每一次调仓的收益加起来，应该等于什么？答案是期权的理论值。单独一次调仓可能赚也可能亏，很多次加起来，平均收回的是理论值。讲义说这个过程是一种统计套利。第一节把十周逐周做一遍，核对这句话。The lecture notes then ask: if you add up the profit from every adjustment over the whole life of the option, what should the total be? The answer is the option's theoretical value. A single adjustment can gain or lose; added up over many adjustments, the average amount recovered is the theoretical value. The lecture notes call this process a form of statistical arbitrage. Section 1 goes through the ten weeks one by one to check this claim.

一十周逐周重对冲1Ten weeks of rehedging, week by week

每周末看一次新的 delta，买卖标的把仓位拉回 delta 中性；第 10 周到期，把所有东西平掉。At the end of each week, look at the new delta and trade the underlying to bring the position back to delta neutral. In week 10 the option expires and everything is closed out.

钱还是按第〇节说的「张 × 点」记：卖 4 张、价格 99.15，记 +396.60（39,660 美元）。每一笔现金流从发生那天存到到期，按 5% 单利计息：第 1 周的 +396.60 存 63 天，利息是 396.60 × 63/365 × 5% ＝ +3.42。Money is still counted in "contracts × points" as in section 0: selling 4 contracts at 99.15 is recorded as +396.60 ($39,660). Each cash flow earns 5% simple interest from the day it occurs until expiration: week 1's +396.60 is held for 63 days, so its interest is 396.60 × 63/365 × 5% = +3.42.

下面每周记一次账。「按理论值记账的合计」是把 100 张 call 按这一周的理论值（42.56% 算）计价，再加上卖空的标的、现金和已经记下的利息，得到的账面盈亏；本节后面细说。Below, the books are updated once a week. "Marked-to-theoretical total" values the 100 calls at this week's theoretical value (at 42.56%), then adds the short underlying, the cash and the interest booked so far. The result is the paper profit or loss; more on it later in this section.

付出的是 5.50，算上利息是 5.55。每张多拿到 1.0575，100 张是 105.75。这就是讲义那句话：对冲过程平均收回的是理论值，买入价低于理论值，差额就变成了现金。The price paid was 5.50, or 5.55 including interest. Each contract collected 1.0575 more, which is 105.75 for 100 contracts. This is the lecture's claim: on average the hedging process recovers the theoretical value, and when you buy below theoretical value, the difference turns into cash.

这里的理论值 6.55，是假设标的之后真的按 42.56% 波动算出来的。讲义接着说：实际波动比 42.56% 大，这张 call 值的比 6.55 多，对冲收回的也更多；实际波动更小，就收回得少。所以更准确的说法是：对冲下来平均收回的，是按标的实际波动率算的理论值。The theoretical value of 6.55 here assumes the underlying really does move with 42.56% volatility afterward. The lecture notes go on: if the realized volatility is above 42.56%, the call is worth more than 6.55 and hedging recovers more; if it is lower, hedging recovers less. So the more precise statement is: what hedging recovers on average is the theoretical value computed at the underlying's realized volatility.

也可以看过程中的账面。按理论值记账，期权每周按当时的理论值计价：入场那一刻，账面已经是 +104.96（100 × (6.5496 − 5.50)）。之后十周，账面在 +20.79（第 4 周）和 +145.31（第 9 周）之间晃，到期落在 +105.75。到期时期权只值内在价值，账面上的数全部变成了现金。账面会晃，是因为每周只调一次，一周里标的走多远是随机的。第二节讲调得更勤会怎样。You can also follow the paper P&L along the way. Marked to theoretical value, the option is valued each week at that week's theoretical value: at the moment of entry the books already show +104.96 (100 × (6.5496 − 5.50)). Over the next ten weeks the paper total swings between +20.79 (week 4) and +145.31 (week 9) and ends at +105.75 at expiration. At expiration the option is worth only its intrinsic value, and the whole paper amount has become cash. The total swings because the position is adjusted only once a week, and how far the underlying moves within a week is random. Section 2 covers what happens when you adjust more often.

一场赛跑A race

讲义把按理论值做期权交易比作一场赛跑。一方是时间流逝让期权的价值变少（2.2 的 theta），另一方是动态对冲不断产生的现金。价格低于理论值时，对冲那一方赢，应该做买方；价格高于理论值时，时间那一方赢，应该做卖方；价格等于理论值，打平。The lecture notes compare trading options against theoretical value to a race. One side is the passage of time, which reduces the option's value (theta, from 2.2); the other side is the cash that dynamic hedging keeps generating. When the price is below theoretical value, hedging wins and you should be the buyer. When the price is above theoretical value, time wins and you should be the seller. When the price equals theoretical value, it is a tie.

讲义还算了一个数：波动率取 36.40% 时，这张 call 的理论值正好是 5.50。标的实际的波动超过 36.40%，这样对冲下来平均是赚的；不到 36.40%，平均是亏的。这个不赚不赔的波动率，就是 5.50 这个价格对应的隐含波动率，4.2 从这里接着讲。The lecture notes also compute one more number: at 36.40% volatility, this call's theoretical value is exactly 5.50. If the underlying's realized volatility is above 36.40%, hedging this way makes money on average; below 36.40%, it loses on average. This break-even volatility is the implied volatility of the 5.50 price. 4.2 continues from here.

讲义这条十周路径，按每周的涨跌量出来的实际波动率约 40.7%，比 42.56% 低。按 40.7% 算，这张 call 值 6.24，100 张对冲下来平均应该收回约 75（含利息）；这一条实际是 105.75，多了 31。每周只调一次，单独一条路径离平均差一两百很常见（第二节：一个标准差 ±195），要看平均，得看很多条路径，这是第二节的模拟。Measured from the weekly moves, this ten-week lecture path has a realized volatility of about 40.7%, lower than 42.56%. At 40.7% the call is worth 6.24, so hedging 100 contracts should recover about 75 on average (with interest); this path actually produced 105.75, 31 more. With one adjustment a week, a single path landing one or two hundred away from the average is common (section 2: one standard deviation is ±195). To see the average you need many paths, which is the simulation in section 2.

二对冲频率与轮盘赌2Hedging frequency and roulette

讲义的十周只调了十次。调得更勤，平均赚的一样多，每一次的结果离平均更近。讲义讲价差（spread）时用轮盘赌说明：分散不改变平均，只缩小风险。同一个道理放到对冲频率上也成立。The lecture's ten weeks had only ten adjustments. Adjusting more often earns the same amount on average, and each outcome lands closer to the average. When the lecture notes discuss spreads, they use roulette to show that diversification does not change the average and only reduces risk. The same logic holds for hedging frequency.

讲义的轮盘赌Roulette in the lecture notes

美国的轮盘有 38 个号：1 到 36，再加 0 和 00。押一个号，押中拿 36 美元，押不中什么也没有。一注的理论价值是 36 ÷ 38 ≈ 0.95 美元。赌场一注卖 1.00，每 1 美元多收 2/38 ≈ 5.3 美分（讲义写约 5%）。An American roulette wheel has 38 numbers: 1 to 36, plus 0 and 00. Bet on one number: if it hits you get $36, otherwise nothing. The theoretical value of one bet is 36 ÷ 38 ≈ $0.95. The casino sells each bet for 1.00, collecting an extra 2/38 ≈ 5.3 cents per dollar (the lecture notes say about 5%).

前两种情形，长期平均一样，风险差一倍：一个人押 2,000，一局最多赔 70,000；分给两个人押，最多赔 34,000。押满 38 个号，每一局都稳赚。讲义的结论：做价差既保住理论上的优势，又降低风险（3.1、3.2 讲价差）。In the first two cases the long-run average is the same, but the risk differs by a factor of two: with one player betting 2,000, the casino can lose up to 70,000 in one round; split between two players, at most 34,000. With all 38 numbers covered, the casino wins every round. The lecture's conclusion: trading spreads keeps the theoretical edge and lowers the risk (spreads are covered in 3.1 and 3.2).

下面是本页把同一个道理用到对冲频率上。对冲的每一次调仓，都像赌场多接下一注小的。每一段时间里，期权比买入价多出来的那一点价值被一点点收回来；调得越勤，注越多、越小，最后的结果越贴近平均。完全不对冲，就像把整笔钱押在一个号上：只看到期那一刻标的在哪。Below, this page applies the same logic to hedging frequency. Each adjustment in a hedge is like the casino taking one more small bet. In each period, a bit of the value by which the option exceeds its purchase price is recovered; the more often you adjust, the more and smaller the bets, and the closer the final result is to the average. Not hedging at all is like putting the whole sum on one number: all that matters is where the underlying is at the moment of expiration.

四种频率的平均都在 105 到 106，就是 105 加上 70 天利息的 106.01（第一节）。不同的是散开的宽度。每周调一次，一个标准差是 ±195，超过四分之一的路径最后是亏的；每小时调一次，±35，亏钱的不到百分之一。对冲次数乘 5（每周到每天），宽度缩到 1/2.17；再乘 7（每天到每小时），缩到 1/2.59。√5 ≈ 2.24，√7 ≈ 2.65：宽度按对冲次数的平方根缩小，又是 1.2 讲过的平方根。The mean at all four frequencies is between 105 and 106: 105 plus 70 days of interest is 106.01 (section 1). What differs is the spread. Adjusting weekly, one standard deviation is ±195 and more than a quarter of the paths end with a loss; adjusting hourly, it is ±35 and fewer than one percent lose money. Multiplying the number of hedges by 5 (weekly to daily) shrinks the width to 1/2.17; multiplying again by 7 (daily to hourly) shrinks it to 1/2.59. √5 ≈ 2.24 and √7 ≈ 2.65: the width shrinks with the square root of the number of hedges, the same square root covered in 1.2.

不对冲那一行平均也是 106。平均一样，是因为模拟里标的平均按利率涨；标的平均涨得更多或更少，不对冲的平均跟着变，对冲的几乎不变。单看一次，不对冲有 68% 的路径是亏的：57% 的路径到期价在 100 以下，550 连本带息全亏；少数路径大赚。它就是一个人把 2,000 押在一个号上。The no-hedge row also averages 106. The average is the same because in the simulation the underlying rises at the interest rate on average. If the underlying rises more or less on average, the no-hedge average moves with it while the hedged averages barely change. Taken one at a time, 68% of the no-hedge paths lose money: in 57% of the paths the price at expiration is below 100, and the 550 plus interest is lost entirely; a few paths make a lot. It is the single player putting 2,000 on one number.

讲义那条十周路径得到 105.75，离模拟的平均 105.46 只差 0.29。按上面的模拟，每周调一次，有 28% 的路径离平均超过 ±195。讲义这条路径碰巧离平均很近。The lecture's ten-week path produced 105.75, only 0.29 away from the simulated mean of 105.46. In the simulation above, with weekly adjustments, 28% of the paths end more than ±195 from the mean. The lecture path happened to land close to the mean.

对冲频率不改变平均赚多少，平均由买入价和理论值之差决定。它改变的是这一次的结果离平均有多远。赌场多接小注，是为了不被一注打垮；交易员调仓调得勤，是为了让这一次的结果贴近 105。代价是买卖次数多，每一笔都要付手续费和买卖价差，上面的模拟没有算这笔钱。Hedging frequency does not change how much you make on average; the average is set by the difference between the purchase price and the theoretical value. What it changes is how far this one result lands from the average. A casino takes many small bets so that no single bet can wipe it out; a trader adjusts often so that this one result stays close to 105. The cost is more trades, and every trade pays commissions and the bid-ask spread. The simulation above does not include that cost.

什么时候调、用什么调When to adjust, and with what

讲义列了两种有纪律的做法：按固定时间调（每周末、每天开盘或收盘、每小时），或者仓位的 delta 偏到事先定好的数就调（200、500、1,000 delta）。凭感觉调，比如看图形，就离开了定价模型。The lecture notes list two disciplined approaches: adjust at fixed times (each weekend, each day at the open or close, each hour), or adjust when the position's delta drifts to a preset amount (200, 500, 1,000 deltas). Adjusting by feel, for example by reading charts, means leaving the pricing model behind.

用标的调仓，只改 delta，gamma、theta、vega、rho 都不变，这也是定价模型假设的调法。用别的期权调仓，会把 gamma、vega 这些一起改掉，仓位的风险跟着变大或变小。讲义建议新手先用标的调。Adjusting with the underlying changes only delta; gamma, theta, vega and rho stay the same. This is also how the pricing model assumes you adjust. Adjusting with other options changes gamma, vega and the rest at the same time, so the position's risk grows or shrinks along with it. The lecture notes advise beginners to adjust with the underlying first.

为什么几乎不赚不赔Why it nearly breaks even

第一节的结论：对冲下来，拿到的平均是按实际波动算的理论值。1.2 量出 TSLA 这 24 天的实际波动率（那一节叫未来波动率，事后量出来的那个）是 45.8%；买入时市场价 21.05 对应的隐含波动率是 46.3%。The conclusion from section 1: what hedging collects on average is the theoretical value at realized volatility. 1.2 measured TSLA's realized volatility over these 24 days (that node calls it future volatility, the one measured after the fact) at 45.8%; the implied volatility of the 21.05 market price at purchase was 46.3%.

五不对冲赌的是什么5What you bet on without a hedge

同一天买入同一行权价的 350 put，也按 delta 对冲，和 call 放在一起比。On the same day, buy the 350 put at the same strike, hedge it by delta as well, and compare it with the call.

4/13 收盘 350 put 的中间价 17.40（买一 17.35，卖一 17.45），delta −0.4426。put 的 delta 是负的，对冲要买股票：买 44 股，之后每天收盘按新的 delta 调。不派息时按模型，put 的 delta 等于 call 的 delta 减 1，这一天 0.5574 − 1 ＝ −0.4426。At the 4/13 close the 350 put's mid price is 17.40 (bid 17.35, ask 17.45) and its delta is −0.4426. A put's delta is negative, so hedging means buying stock: buy 44 shares, then adjust at each day's close to the new delta. With no dividend, under the model a put's delta equals the call's delta minus 1; on this day 0.5574 − 1 = −0.4426.

对冲以后 call 和 put 几乎一样，是因为同一行权价、同一到期日的 call 和 put，gamma 相同，vega 也相同（2.3），把方向对冲掉以后，剩下的是同一件事：买入价里的隐含波动率对标的的实际波动率。call 这边是隐含 46.3% 对实际 45.8%，put 这边是隐含 46.1% 对实际 45.8%。不对冲时一个赚一个亏，差出的 6,866 全部来自方向：TSLA 这 24 天涨了 19.9%。After hedging the call and the put look almost the same, because a call and a put with the same strike and the same expiration have the same gamma and the same vega (2.3). Once direction is hedged away, what is left is the same thing for both: the implied volatility in the purchase price against the underlying's realized volatility. For the call it is 46.3% implied against 45.8% realized; for the put, 46.1% implied against 45.8% realized. Without a hedge one makes money and the other loses, and the 6,866 difference comes entirely from direction: TSLA rose 19.9% over these 24 days.

仓位里每一条腿的 delta 乘以张数再相加，包括以前所有的调仓。The delta of each leg in the position times its number of contracts, summed, including every earlier adjustment.

定期调仓、让仓位一直保持 delta 中性的整个过程。The whole process of adjusting at regular intervals so the position stays delta neutral.

单独一次不保证赚钱，按统计平均一定赚钱的交易。A trade that does not guarantee a profit on any single try but makes money on a statistical average.
