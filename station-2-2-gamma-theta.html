gamma 与 thetaGamma and theta

2.1 讲了 delta：标的动 1 美元，期权跟着动多少。这一节讲两件事：delta 自己变得多快（gamma），持有期权每天流失多少时间价值（theta）。模型把这两个数绑在一个固定比例上。案例还是 2026-04-13 收盘买入的 TSLA 5/15 到期 350 call。2.1 covered delta: when the underlying moves 1 dollar, how much the option moves with it. This node covers two things: how fast delta itself changes (gamma), and how much time value an option loses each day you hold it (theta). The model ties these two numbers together in a fixed ratio. The case is still the TSLA 350 call expiring 5/15, bought at the 2026-04-13 close.

〇delta 自己会变0Delta changes on its own

4/13 收盘买入时，报价快照里这张 350 call 的 delta 是 0.557。第二天 TSLA 涨了 11.96 美元，delta 变成 0.651；第三天又涨 27.61，delta 变成 0.807。At entry at the 4/13 close, this 350 call's delta in the quote snapshot was 0.557. The next day TSLA rose 11.96 dollars and delta became 0.651; the day after, it rose another 27.61 and delta became 0.807.

1.1 算过：整段持有下来，这张 call 实际跟上了 TSLA 涨幅的 0.734，比入场时的 0.557 大，原因就是 delta 一路在变大。这一节先量它变得有多快。1.1 worked it out: over the whole holding period, this call actually captured 0.734 of TSLA's gain, more than the 0.557 at entry, because delta kept growing along the way. This node first measures how fast it changes.

一gamma 在哪里大1Where gamma is large

同一天、同一个 TSLA 价格，不同行权价、不同到期日的期权，gamma 差好几倍。下图是 4/13 收盘报价里的四个到期日，TSLA 都是 352.36。On the same day and at the same TSLA price, options with different strikes and expirations can differ in gamma several times over. The chart below shows four expirations from the 4/13 closing quotes, all with TSLA at 352.36.

越近行权价越大。每条线都在平值附近（352.5 到 360 这几档）最高，往两边落下去。离平值远的期权，delta 贴在 0 或 1 附近，标的动一点它几乎不变。Larger closer to the strike. Every line peaks near the money (the 352.5 to 360 strikes) and falls off on both sides. For options far from the money, delta sits near 0 or 1, and a small move in the underlying barely changes it.

越近到期越大。平值附近，2 天到期的 gamma 是 0.0367，32 天的是 0.0084，差 4.4 倍。原因要回到 1.2 的一个标准差（远期价 × 隐含波动率 × √t）：到期前 delta 要从接近 0 爬到接近 1，爬坡大约占几个标准差宽。离到期 32 天，1.2 算过，一个标准差约 48.5 美元；离到期 2 天，平值那档的隐含波动率是 42.5%，一个标准差只有约 11.1 美元。同样的爬升挤进约四分之一的宽度，坡就陡了约 4 倍。√(32/2) ＝ 4；两个到期日的隐含波动率不同（46.3% 对 42.5%），再乘 46.3 ÷ 42.5，是 4.4 倍，和 gamma 的倍数对得上。Larger closer to expiration. Near the money, the 2-day gamma is 0.0367 and the 32-day gamma is 0.0084, 4.4 times apart. The reason goes back to the standard deviation from 1.2 (forward price × implied volatility × √t): before expiration, delta has to climb from near 0 to near 1, and the climb spans a few standard deviations. With 32 days to go, as 1.2 worked out, one standard deviation is about 48.5 dollars; with 2 days to go, the at-the-money strike's implied volatility is 42.5%, and one standard deviation is only about 11.1 dollars. The same climb squeezed into about a quarter of the width makes the slope about 4 times steeper. √(32/2) = 4; the two expirations have different implied volatilities (46.3% vs. 42.5%), so multiply by 46.3 ÷ 42.5 to get 4.4 times, which matches the ratio of the gammas.

离平值远的地方反过来：320 这档，2 天到期的 gamma 只有 0.0022，比 32 天的 0.0059 还小。32 美元对 2 天的期权约是三个标准差，delta 早已贴在 1 附近不动了。这种反转节点 6 细讲。Far from the money it is the other way around: at the 320 strike, the 2-day gamma is only 0.0022, smaller than the 32-day 0.0059. For a 2-day option, 32 dollars is about three standard deviations, and delta has long since settled near 1. Node 6 covers this reversal in detail.

theta 也一样，平值最大、越近到期越大：2 天到期的平值 call 每天流失 1.10，32 天的 0.32。讲义的说法是平值期权的 gamma、theta 都比同等的实值、虚值期权大。Theta follows the same pattern, largest at the money and larger closer to expiration: the 2-day at-the-money call loses 1.10 a day, the 32-day one 0.32. In the lecture notes' words, at-the-money options have larger gamma and theta than comparable in-the-money and out-of-the-money options.

二theta：时间价值每天流失多少2Theta: how much time value is lost each day

1.1 讲过，期权价格 ＝ 内在价值 ＋ 时间价值。入场时 350 call 值 21.05：内在价值 352.36 − 350 ＝ 2.36，剩下 18.69 是时间价值，付的是「到期前 TSLA 还可能走多远」。到期那一刻时间价值归零，theta 量的是它每天流失多快。As 1.1 explained, option price = intrinsic value + time value. At entry the 350 call was worth 21.05: intrinsic value 352.36 − 350 = 2.36, and the remaining 18.69 is time value, paid for "how far TSLA might still move before expiration". At expiration time value goes to zero; theta measures how fast it drains each day.

Θ ＝ 只过一天、其他不变时，期权价值的变化Θ = change in option value when one day passes and nothing else changes
标的价格、波动率、利率都不动，时间往前走一天，期权少值多少The underlying price, volatility and interest rate stay put and time moves forward one day: how much less the option is worth

讲义的例子：期权值 5.00，theta 是 −.05，过一天 4.95，两天 4.90，三天 4.85。报价快照里 350 call 的 theta 是 −0.319：其他不变，过一天，21.05 变成约 20.73。The lecture notes' example: an option worth 5.00 with theta −.05 is worth 4.95 after one day, 4.90 after two, 4.85 after three. In the quote snapshot the 350 call's theta is −0.319: with everything else unchanged, after one day 21.05 becomes about 20.73.

买期权的人 theta 为负，每天都在流失；卖期权的人 theta 为正，每天都在收进来。讲义举过一个例外，要三个条件同时满足：深度实值，几乎肯定会被行权；欧式，只能在到期那天行权，今天拿不到内在价值；买的时候当场付全款（讲义叫股票型结算，美国的期权都这样；有的交易所买期权先不付钱，只按每天的涨跌结算盈亏）。这样的期权今天的价值是到期价值的折现，比内在价值还低，过一天少折现一天，价值反而涨一点，theta 为正。TSLA 的期权是美式的，随时可以行权，本页的期权都不属于这种。An option buyer's theta is negative, losing value every day; an option seller's theta is positive, taking it in every day. The lecture notes give one exception, which needs three conditions at once: deep in the money, almost certain to be exercised; European, exercisable only on the expiration date, so the intrinsic value cannot be collected today; and paid for in full at purchase (the lecture notes call this stock-type settlement, which is how all US options work; on some exchanges you pay nothing up front for an option and only settle each day's gains and losses). Such an option is worth today the discounted value at expiration, less than its intrinsic value; with each day that passes there is one day less of discounting, so its value actually rises a little, and theta is positive. TSLA's options are American and can be exercised at any time, so none of the options on this page are of this kind.

时间价值大致按剩余天数的平方根缩小，原因和 1.2 一样：还能走多远按 √t 算。剩一半时间，还剩约 √½ ≈ 71%；剩四分之一时间，还剩约一半。平方根曲线越靠近 0 越陡，所以 theta 越近到期越大。Time value shrinks roughly with the square root of the days left, for the same reason as in 1.2: how far the price can still move scales with √t. 三为什么 gamma 和 theta 成比例3Why gamma and theta are proportional

gamma 让买方在标的大动时多赚一点，theta 让买方每天付一点。模型把两者锁在一个固定比例上。先算大动一天 gamma 多赚多少。Gamma lets the buyer make a bit more when the underlying moves a lot; theta makes the buyer pay a bit every day. The model locks the two in a fixed ratio. First, work out how much extra gamma earns on a big day.

gamma 多赚的那一块The extra piece gamma earns

标的从 S 动到 S ＋ ΔS，delta 一路从 Δ 变到 Δ ＋ Γ × ΔS。这一路上 delta 的平均值是 Δ ＋ ½ × Γ × ΔS。期权价值的变化等于「标的动了多少 × 一路上的平均 delta」：As the underlying moves from S to S + ΔS, delta goes from Δ to Δ + Γ × ΔS along the way. The average delta along the way is Δ + ½ × Γ × ΔS. The change in option value equals "how far the underlying moved × the average delta along the way":

期权价值的变化 ≈ Δ × ΔS ＋ ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²Change in option value ≈ Δ × ΔS + ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²
按起点的 delta 算的部分 ＋ delta 一路变大（变小）多出来的部分The part from the starting delta + the extra from delta growing (or shrinking) along the way

讲义的例子：股票 49 涨到 51，50 call 值 2.67，delta 50，gamma 5。到 51 时 delta 是 50 ＋ 2 × 5 ＝ 60，平均 55，新价值 2.67 ＋ 2 × 0.55 ＝ 3.77。用上面的式子：2.67 ＋ 2 × 0.50 ＋ ½ × 5 × 2² ÷ 100 ＝ 2.67 ＋ 1.00 ＋ 0.10 ＝ 3.77，两种算法一样。2.4 会用同一个式子估整个组合。The lecture notes' example: the stock rises from 49 to 51, and the 50 call is worth 2.67, with delta 50 and gamma 5. At 51, delta is 50 + 2 × 5 = 60, the average is 55, and the new value is 2.67 + 2 × 0.55 = 3.77. With the formula above: 2.67 + 2 × 0.50 + ½ × 5 × 2² ÷ 100 = 2.67 + 1.00 + 0.10 = 3.77, the same either way. 2.4 uses the same formula to estimate a whole portfolio.

第一项是方向。2.1 讲过，卖出 Δ 股标的就能把它抵掉，这叫对冲。对冲之后只剩 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²，它有两个特点：The first term is direction. As 2.1 explained, selling Δ shares of the underlying offsets it; this is called hedging. After hedging only ½ × Γ × (ΔS)² is left, and it has two properties:

  • ΔS 平方以后总是正的：涨也赚，跌也赚。ΔS squared is always positive: you make money on a rise and on a fall.
  • 按幅度的平方增长：动的幅度翻倍，赚的变成 4 倍。It grows with the square of the size: twice the move earns 4 times as much.

平均一天赚多少How much it earns on an average day

明天动多少事先不知道。按 1.2，一天的一个标准差是 S × σ ÷ 16。1.2 算标准差的办法是把每天离 0 的距离平方、求平均、再开方；反过来，ΔS 平方的平均值就是一个标准差的平方。这一点对任何形状的分布都成立。所以 gamma 平均每天赚：How far the price will move tomorrow is not known in advance. From 1.2, one standard deviation for a day is S × σ ÷ 16. 1.2 computed the standard deviation by squaring each day's distance from 0, averaging, and taking the square root; turned around, the average of ΔS squared is the square of one standard deviation. This holds for a distribution of any shape. So on an average day gamma earns:

平均一天 ＝ ½ × Γ × (S × σ ÷ 16)²Average day = ½ × Γ × (S × σ ÷ 16)²
把一个标准差当作这一天的 ΔS 代进 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²Plug one standard deviation into ½ × Γ × (ΔS)² as the day's ΔS
350 call 入场时：½ × 0.00820 × (352.36 × 46.3% ÷ 16)² ＝ ½ × 0.00820 × 10.20² ≈ 0.426For the 350 call at entry: ½ × 0.00820 × (352.36 × 46.3% ÷ 16)² = ½ × 0.00820 × 10.20² ≈ 0.426

这个平均值是怎么凑出来的，用 2000 个模拟的交易日看：每一天从钟形分布里抽一个涨跌。gamma 那天赚的钱按「动了几个标准差」的平方算：动 1 个标准差正好赚平均值那么多，动 2 个赚 4 倍，动半个只赚四分之一。图里把每天付的 theta（本页叫租金）定成这个平均值，看收入和租金怎么相抵。To see how this average comes together, look at 2000 simulated trading days: each day draws a move from a bell-shaped distribution. What gamma earns that day goes with the square of "how many standard deviations it moved": a 1 standard deviation move earns exactly the average, 2 earns 4 times as much, half a standard deviation earns only a quarter. The chart sets the theta paid each day (this page calls it rent) equal to this average, to show how income and rent offset.

公平的租金A fair rent

如果 theta 比这个平均值小，买入期权再对冲，平均每天稳赚：gamma 收进来的多于付出去的。人人都去买，期权价格被抬高，theta 跟着变大，直到每天的租金等于 gamma 的平均收入。反过来卖方稳赚，价格被压低。If theta were smaller than this average, buying the option and hedging would make money on average every day: gamma would bring in more than goes out. Everyone would buy, the option price would be pushed up, and theta would grow with it, until the daily rent equaled gamma's average income. The other way around, sellers would make money for sure and the price would be pushed down.

上面算的是一个交易日。一年约 256 个交易日，而 16² ＝ 256，所以一年的 gamma 平均收入是 ½ × Γ × (S × σ ÷ 16)² × 256 ＝ ½ × σ² × S² × Γ，16 正好消掉。所以在模型里：The calculation above is for one trading day. A year has about 256 trading days, and 16² = 256, so a year's average gamma income is ½ × Γ × (S × σ ÷ 16)² × 256 = ½ × σ² × S² × Γ, and the 16 cancels exactly. So in the model:

Θ ≈ −½ × σ² × S² × Γ
一年要付的租金 ＝ ½ × 波动率² × 股价² × gamma；换成每天，除以一年的天数Rent paid per year = ½ × volatility² × stock price² × gamma; for a day, divide by the number of days in a year

这就是买 gamma 就要付 theta：gamma 越大，租金越贵，比例由 σ² × S² 定。本页把 theta 叫租金：持有期权，每天为「标的可能大动」付的钱。This is buy gamma, pay theta: the larger the gamma, the higher the rent, in a ratio set by σ² × S². This page calls theta rent: what you pay each day you hold an option, for the chance that the underlying makes a big move.

The hedged position is long one call (paying 21.05) and short 0.5574 shares (receiving 196.41), leaving 175.36 dollars of net cash, which earns 0.021 a day at 4.3%. The model requires this position to break even on an average day, so theta has to offset both gamma's average income and this interest: 0.299 + 0.021 = 0.320, and the quote snapshot gives 0.319. 四逐日账本：买 gamma 付 theta4Daily ledger: buy gamma, pay theta

买入 350 call，每天收盘按新的 delta 调一次股票，把方向对冲掉（4.1 讲具体怎么调）。这样每一天只剩两笔账：gamma 赚的 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)²，和付掉的租金。Buy the 350 call and adjust the stock once at each close to the new delta, hedging out the direction (4.1 covers how to adjust). Each day then leaves only two entries: what gamma earned, ½ × Γ × (ΔS)², and the rent paid.

回本的门槛是一个标准差The break-even threshold is one standard deviation

gamma 赚的等于租金，就是 ½ × Γ × (ΔS)² ＝ ½ × Γ × (S × σ ÷ 16)²。两边的 ½ × Γ 消掉：Gamma's earnings equal the rent when ½ × Γ × (ΔS)² = ½ × Γ × (S × σ ÷ 16)². The ½ × Γ on both sides cancels:

门槛和 gamma 大小无关：gamma 大的期权赚得多，租金也同比例贵The threshold does not depend on the size of gamma: options with large gamma earn more, and their rent is proportionally higher

横盘时买方慢慢亏When the price goes sideways, the buyer slowly loses

4/24 到 5/6 这 9 个交易日，TSLA 每天都没动到一个标准差，最大的一天 2.37%。第一种账本在这 9 天里天天付租：gamma 只赚了 1.16，租金付了 4.49，净亏 3.34。In the 9 trading days from 4/24 to 5/6, TSLA never moved one standard deviation in a day; the largest day was 2.37%. 这 9 天 TSLA 其实从 373.69 涨到 398.62，涨了 6.67%。如果这 6.67% 在一天里走完，是 2.31 个标准差，gamma 那一天就赚 2.55，是当天租金的 5.3 倍。gamma 只按每一天动多大算钱，不看累计走了多远。横盘和慢慢走，对买方是一样的：每天付租，等不来大动的日子。Over those 9 days TSLA actually rose from 373.69 to 398.62, up 6.67%. Had that 6.67% happened in one day, it would be 2.31 standard deviations, and gamma would have earned 2.55 that day, 5.3 times that day's rent. Gamma pays only by how big each day's move is, not by how far the price has gone in total. Going sideways and drifting slowly are the same for the buyer: rent every day, and the big days never come.

五收租还是付租5Collecting rent or paying rent

gamma 为正，theta 就为负：在付租，等标的大动。gamma 为负，theta 就为正：在收租，怕标的大动。看一个就知道另一个。With positive gamma, theta is negative: you are paying rent and waiting for a big move. With negative gamma, theta is positive: you are collecting rent and afraid of a big move. Knowing one tells you the other.

In the lecture notes, 1 lot of the underlying is 100 shares, the same number of shares as one option contract covers (1.1); in whole number format its delta is 100, and its gamma and theta are both 0. Adding up the legs: delta −10 × 73 + 7 × 100 = −30; gamma −10 × 3.3 = −33; theta −10 × (−.0387) = +0.387. If the market does not move, the position collects 0.387 a day.
