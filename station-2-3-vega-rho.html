〇vega 是什么0What vega is

1.2 讲过，隐含波动率是从期权市价反推出来的 σ，相当于市场给波动率的报价。这个报价每天都在变，期权价格也跟着变。vega 量的就是这一块。As 1.2 explained, implied volatility is the σ backed out from an option's market price, in effect the market's quote for volatility. This quote changes every day, and the option price changes with it. Vega measures this piece.

定义Definition

vega ＝ 隐含波动率变 1 个点时，期权价格变多少vega = how much the option price changes when implied volatility changes by 1 point
「1 个点」是 46.3% 变成 47.3%，不是 46.3% 再乘 1.01。和 delta 一样按每股报，乘 100 才是一张"1 point" means 46.3% becoming 47.3%, not 46.3% times 1.01. Like delta it is quoted per share; multiply by 100 for one contract

vega 永远是正的Vega is always positive

隐含波动率高，意思是市场认为到期时的分布更宽（1.2）。分布变宽，call 的好结果更好：涨得更多的那些情形多赚。坏结果不会更坏：跌得再多，call 也只是作废，最多亏掉买它花的钱。put 反过来，也是好的一头变大、坏的一头有底。所以不管 call 还是 put，隐含波动率升，价格都升。买期权就是持有正的 vega，卖期权就是负的 vega。Higher implied volatility means the market sees a wider distribution at expiration (1.2). With a wider distribution, a call's good outcomes get better: the cases where the price rises further make more money. Its bad outcomes do not get worse: however far the price falls, the call just expires worthless, and at most you lose what you paid for it. A put is the mirror image: its good end also grows, and its bad end has a floor. So for calls and puts alike, when implied volatility rises, the price rises. Buying options means holding positive vega; selling options means negative vega.

同一行权价的 call 和 put，vega 相同A call and a put at the same strike have the same vega

1.1 用 put-call parity 从 call 和 put 的报价反解利率：call 减 put 只取决于远期价、行权价和利率，里面没有 σ。σ 变了，call 和 put 必须变一样多，这个差才能不变。讲义 2016-08-26 的 S&P 500 期权表里（S&P 500 是美国 500 家大公司股票的指数），波动率从 10% 提到 13%，同一行权价的 call 和 put 涨得完全一样：2075 都是 6.21，2175 都是 10.14，2275 都是 5.77。报价快照也只给一列 vega，call 和 put 共用。1.1 used put-call parity to back out the interest rate from call and put quotes: call minus put depends only on the forward price, the strike and the interest rate, with no σ in it. When σ changes, the call and the put must change by the same amount for that difference to stay put. In the lecture notes' S&P 500 option table from 2016-08-26 (the S&P 500 is an index of the stocks of 500 large US companies), raising volatility from 10% to 13% raises the call and the put at the same strike by exactly the same amount: 6.21 for both at 2075, 10.14 for both at 2175, 5.77 for both at 2275. The quote snapshot also gives only one vega column, shared by calls and puts.

三vega 三定律：第一条和第二条3Three rules of vega: the first and the second

讲义给了 vega 的三条规律。前两条比较同一到期日、不同行权价的期权，第三条比较同一行权价、不同到期日的期权，第四节讲。The lecture notes give three rules about vega. The first two compare options with the same expiration and different strikes; the third compares options with the same strike and different expirations, covered in section 4.

  1. 第一条按美元算（讲义叫「点数」），平值期权对波动率变化最敏感，同等的实值和虚值期权都比不上它。Rule 1 In dollar terms (the lecture notes say "points"), an at-the-money option is the most sensitive to changes in volatility, more than equivalent in-the-money and out-of-the-money options.
  2. 第二条按百分比算，虚值期权对波动率变化最敏感，同等的实值和平值期权都比不上它。Rule 2 In percentage terms, an out-of-the-money option is the most sensitive to changes in volatility, more than equivalent in-the-money and at-the-money options.
  3. 第三条长期期权对波动率变化，永远比同等的短期期权敏感。Rule 3 A long-term option is always more sensitive to changes in volatility than an equivalent short-term option.

「同等」指标的相同、都是 call 或都是 put，只差行权价或只差到期日。讲义三条都用了「永远」。"Equivalent" means the same underlying and both calls or both puts, differing only in strike or only in expiration. All three rules in the lecture notes use the word "always".

为什么平值的 vega 最大Why at-the-money vega is largest

1.1 的到期损益图是一条折线：call 在行权价以下值 0，以上每高 1 美元多值 1 美元。1.1's payoff diagram at expiration is a kinked line: a call is worth 0 below the strike, and each dollar above it is worth one more dollar.

如果到期价值是一条直线，分布变宽时，多出来的上涨和多出来的下跌一正一负，平均值不变，波动率对价格没有影响。股票就是这样：股票的 vega 是 0。If the value at expiration were a straight line, a wider distribution would add extra upside and extra downside that cancel out, the average would not change, and volatility would have no effect on the price. That is how stock works: the vega of stock is 0.

折线在行权价那里拐了个弯。拐点以下再跌也只是 0，拐点以上多涨的全算数。分布变宽带来的好处，全部来自拐点附近那一段。拐点落在分布中间最厚的一带，附近的可能结果最多，好处最大，这就是平值期权。行权价离中心越远，那里的分布越薄，好处越小。The line has a bend at the strike. Below the kink a further fall still only gives 0; above it every extra gain counts. All the benefit of a wider distribution comes from the stretch near the kink. When the kink sits in the thickest part in the middle of the distribution, the most possible outcomes are nearby and the benefit is largest; that is the at-the-money option. The further the strike is from the center, the thinner the distribution there and the smaller the benefit.

为什么虚值的百分比最大Why out-of-the-money options gain the most in percentage terms

先说期货First, futures

期货是一份合约：现在约定一个价格，到将来某一天按这个价格买卖一定数量的标的。签约时双方都不付货款。交易所每天收盘定一个结算价，按今天和昨天结算价的差，把盈亏从亏的一方划给赚的一方。为了保证亏的一方付得出，双方都要先在清算所（交易所负责收付钱的机构）存一笔押金，叫保证金。期货价格就是约定的那个将来的价格，1.1 讲的远期价是它的理论值。A futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell a set amount of the underlying on a future date at a price agreed now. Neither side pays for the goods when the contract is signed. At each close the exchange sets a settlement price, and the difference between today's and yesterday's settlement prices is moved as profit or loss from the losing side to the winning side. To make sure the losing side can pay, both sides first deposit money with the clearinghouse (the body that collects and pays money for the exchange); this deposit is called margin. The futures price is that agreed future price, and the forward price from 1.1 is its theoretical value.

两种结算方式Two ways to settle

股票型结算Stock-type settlement

讲义的例子：以 65 买 100 股，当场付 6,500。涨到 75，账上多了 1,000，卖掉之前是未实现盈亏，卖掉才实现。The lecture notes' example: buy 100 shares at 65, paying 6,500 on the spot. At 75 the account shows 1,000 more; until you sell, that is an unrealized gain, and selling realizes it.

钱在买入那一刻付出去，一直被占着，占着的钱有利息成本。The money is paid out at the moment of purchase and stays tied up, and tied-up money has an interest cost.

期货型结算Futures-type settlement

讲义的例子：以 2200 买 1 张 S&P 500 期货，每点 250 美元，名义价值 2200 × 250 = 550,000 美元（这张合约代表的市值，不是付出去的钱）。买卖双方当场一分钱不付，只在清算所存保证金。期货涨到 2210，当天就划给你 2,500；跌到 2190，当天就从你账上扣 2,500。The lecture notes' example: buy 1 S&P 500 futures contract at 2200, 250 dollars a point, for a notional value of 2200 × 250 = 550,000 dollars (the market value the contract represents, not money paid out). Neither buyer nor seller pays anything on the spot; they only deposit margin with the clearinghouse. If the futures rise to 2210, 2,500 is credited to you that day; if they fall to 2190, 2,500 is debited from your account that day.

每天按结算价结清，永远没有未实现盈亏，也没有钱被占着。Settled at the settlement price every day: there is never an unrealized gain or loss, and no money is tied up.

讲义还有一条规则：多数市场里，期权跟着标的的结算方式走，期货期权用期货型结算。美国是例外：所有期权，包括期货期权，一律股票型结算，买的时候全额付钱。The lecture notes give one more rule: in most markets, options follow the settlement method of their underlying, so options on futures use futures-type settlement. The US is the exception: all options, including options on futures, use stock-type settlement and are paid for in full at purchase.

In the lecture notes' words: for options on futures with stock-type settlement, as in the US, all options have negative rho.

期货型结算时，买期权当天不付钱，期权本身也像期货一样每天按结算价结清，没有钱被占着，没有东西需要折现，rho 是 0。With futures-type settlement, nothing is paid on the day the option is bought, and the option itself is settled every day at the settlement price like a futures contract, so no money is tied up, there is nothing to discount, and rho is 0.

七分红：通过 delta 起作用7Dividends: they work through delta

股票期权还受分红影响。讲义没有给它单独一个希腊字母：分红的影响用 delta 就能算。Stock options are also affected by dividends. The lecture notes do not give this its own Greek letter: delta is enough to compute the effect of dividends.

1.1 讲过 F = S(1 + r·t) − D。分红多 1 美元，远期价少 1 美元，分布的中心往下挪 1 美元，这和股价跌 1 美元对期权的影响一样。股价跌 1 美元期权变多少，就是 delta。As 1.1 explained, F = S(1 + r·t) − D. One more dollar of dividends means a forward price one dollar lower, moving the center of the distribution down by 1 dollar, which affects the option the same way as the stock falling 1 dollar. How much the option changes when the stock falls 1 dollar is delta.

期权价格变化 ≈ − Δ × 分红变化Change in option price ≈ − Δ × change in dividend
分红提高，就像标的下跌同样的钱：call 的 Δ 为正，所以跌；put 的 Δ 为负，所以涨A higher dividend is like the underlying falling by the same amount: a call's Δ is positive, so it falls; a put's Δ is negative, so it rises

利息和分红方向相反Interest and dividends work in opposite directions

利率升，远期价升，call 涨、put 跌；分红升，远期价降，call 跌、put 涨。讲义的原话：股票期权里，利息和分红对期权价值的影响永远相反。When the rate rises, the forward price rises, calls go up and puts go down; when the dividend rises, the forward price falls, calls go down and puts go up. In the lecture notes' words: for stock options, interest and dividends always have opposite effects on option values.
