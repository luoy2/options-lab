〇conversion：三条腿拼成一条水平线0Conversion: three legs that make a horizontal line

5.1 讲过：买 call、卖 put（同一个行权价、同一个到期日），到期损益和持有股票一样，叫合成多头股票；反过来卖 call、买 put 是合成空头股票。把合成空头和一份真股票放在一起，涨跌就全部抵消。As 5.1 explained, buying a call and selling a put (same strike, same expiration) has the same expiration P&L as holding the stock; it is called a synthetic long stock. The reverse, selling a call and buying a put, is a synthetic short stock. Put a synthetic short together with a share of real stock and the gains and losses cancel completely.

讲义对套利的定义是：同一样东西，或者几乎一样的东西，在两处价格不同，一边买、一边卖，赚中间的差。合成空头股票和真股票就是「几乎一样的东西」，由此有两个交易：The lecture's definition of arbitrage: when the same thing, or nearly the same thing, has different prices in two places, buy on one side, sell on the other, and earn the difference. Synthetic short stock and real stock are "nearly the same thing", which gives two trades:

拿 conversion 来看到期时发生什么。设行权价是 X：Take the conversion and look at what happens at expiration. Let the strike be X:

  • TSLA 到期时在 X 上方：put 没用了，作废；call 的买方会行权，你被指派，按 X 交出股票。TSLA above X at expiration: the put is useless and expires; the call buyer exercises, you are assigned, and you deliver the stock at X.
  • TSLA 到期时在 X 下方：call 作废；你行权手里的 put，按 X 卖出股票。TSLA below X at expiration: the call expires; you exercise the put you hold and sell the stock at X.

两种情况的结果一样：股票按 X 卖掉，手里什么都不剩。今天付多少钱是确定的，到期收多少钱也是确定的，和 TSLA 走到哪里无关。Both cases end the same way: the stock is sold at X and nothing is left. How much you pay today is certain, how much you receive at expiration is certain, and neither depends on where TSLA goes.

一五步推导 put-call parity1Deriving put-call parity in five steps

1.1 用 put-call parity 从 TSLA 的真实报价反解出市场用的利率，340 到 360 几个行权价在 4.1% 到 4.4% 之间，那时只用了结论。这里从 conversion 的每一笔钱出发，把它推出来。1.1 used put-call parity to back out the interest rate the market was using from real TSLA quotes, between 4.1% and 4.4% across strikes from 340 to 360; at that point only the result was used. Here it is derived from each cash flow of a conversion.

讲义的做法是把 conversion 从建仓到到期的每一笔钱分成两栏：贷方记收到的钱，借方记付出的钱。钱放着会生息，所以每一笔今天发生的钱都要带上它到期前的利息。The lecture's method is to split every cash flow of the conversion, from opening to expiration, into two columns: credits record money received and debits record money paid out. Money earns interest while it sits, so every amount that occurs today has to carry its interest until expiration.

利息用两个字母表示。r 是年利率，这里沿用 1.1 反解出的 4.3%；t 是到期时间，以年计，32 天就是 32/365 年。讲义用单利，r·t 就是这段时间的利息占本金的比例，所以一笔钱 C 放到到期，利息是 C·r·t。Interest is written with two letters. r is the annual interest rate, here the 4.3% backed out in 1.1; t is the time to expiration in years, so 32 days is 32/365 years. The lecture notes use simple interest, so r·t is the interest over this period as a fraction of principal, and an amount C held to expiration earns interest of C·r·t.

二combo：call 减 put2Combo: call minus put

交易员把同一行权价、同一到期日的「买 call、卖 put」当成一个整体来报价，叫 combo，价格就是 C − P。Traders quote "buy a call, sell a put" with the same strike and expiration as one unit, called a combo, priced at C − P.

第 ⑤ 步说 combo 等于 (F − X) ÷ (1 ＋ r·t)。F − X 是按远期价算的内在价值，再折回今天。所以讲义说：combo 是内在价值的现值。不是按现价算的 S − X，也不是没折现的 F − X。Step ⑤ says the combo equals (F − X) ÷ (1 + r·t). F − X is the intrinsic value at the forward price, discounted to today. So the lecture notes say: the combo is the present value of the intrinsic value. Not S − X at the current price, and not the undiscounted F − X.

买 combo 等于约好到期按 X 买股票。和今天直接买股票比，X 到期才付，钱多留在手里 32 天，利息归自己；如果股票期间派息，分红拿不到。这两件事让 combo 比 S − X 高出利息、低掉分红。Buying a combo is agreeing to buy the stock at X at expiration. Compared with buying the stock today, X is paid only at expiration, so the money stays with you for 32 more days and the interest is yours; but if the stock pays a dividend in the meantime, you don't get it. These two things put the combo above S − X by the interest and below it by the dividend.

有了 combo，conversion 和 reversal 可以换一种说法：conversion 是按市价卖出 combo、同时买股票；reversal 是买入 combo、同时卖空股票。套的是 combo 的市价和它该值的 (F − X) ÷ (1 ＋ r·t) 之间的差。With the combo, conversion and reversal can be described another way: a conversion sells the combo at the market price and buys the stock at the same time; a reversal buys the combo and sells the stock short at the same time. What they capture is the gap between the combo's market price and what it should be worth, (F − X) ÷ (1 + r·t).

同一个东西，走哪条路买更便宜Same thing, which route is cheaper to buy

parity 还给了第二个用处：想买 call，可以直接买，也可以买 put 再买股票（合成 call）。哪条路便宜，要用实际能成交的那一边的价格来比：买按卖一，卖按买一。Parity also gives a second use: to buy a call you can buy it directly, or buy a put and buy the stock (a synthetic call). To see which route is cheaper, compare using the side of the quote you can actually trade at: buy at the ask, sell at the bid.

450 上的 0.58 是白捡的吗Is the 0.58 at 450 free money?

reversal 里卖出的是 put。450 put 深度实值（TSLA 352.36，行权价 450），它的买方有理由提前行权：今天就按 450 卖出股票，拿到 450 美元现金去生息，比等 32 天划算。节点 9 专门讲这套判据：股票 put 的提前行权靠的是利息。In a reversal the put is the leg you sell. Node 9 covers these criteria in detail: early exercise of a stock put is driven by interest.

这项权利值多少钱，要用模型算。这里用的是二叉树模型：把到期前的 32 天切成 2000 小步，每一步 TSLA 要么涨一格、要么跌一格，一格多大由波动率决定。从到期那一步往回算，美式 put 在每一步都比较两个数，现在行权拿到的 X − S 和继续拿着的价值，取大的；欧式 put 只能继续拿着。两者今天的价值之差，就是提前行权这项权利的价值，图里的蓝色虚线。波动率用报价快照给每个行权价算出的隐含波动率，利率 4.3%。What this right is worth has to be computed with a model. The one used here is a binomial tree model: cut the 32 days before expiration into 2000 small steps; at each step TSLA moves up one notch or down one notch, with the notch size set by volatility. Working back from the expiration step, the American put compares two numbers at every step, the X − S from exercising now and the value of holding on, and takes the larger; the European put can only hold on. The difference between their values today is the value of the early-exercise right, the blue dashed line in the chart. Volatility is the implied volatility the quote snapshot gives for each strike; interest is 4.3%.

五pin risk：到期时恰好停在行权价上5Pin risk: expiring right at the strike

conversion 靠到期时「自己行权 put」或「call 被指派」把股票卖掉。股价收在行权价附近时，这一步会出问题。A conversion sells the stock at expiration by "exercising your own put" or "having the call assigned". When the stock closes near the strike, this step can go wrong.

讲义的问题：持有 100 行权价的 conversion，到期时标的恰好是 100，怎么办？The lecture's question: you hold a conversion at the 100 strike, and at expiration the underlying is exactly 100. What do you do?

  • call 被指派了，股票已经按 100 交出去，就不该再行权 put，否则会多卖一份，变成空头。If the call is assigned, the stock has already been delivered at 100, and you should not exercise the put, or you sell one unit too many and end up short.
  • call 没被指派，就必须行权 put，否则股票留在手里，变成多头。If the call is not assigned, you must exercise the put, or the stock stays with you and you end up long.
  • 有没有被指派，要到到期之后才知道。Whether you were assigned is known only after expiration.

讲义的定义：pin risk 是到期时持有平值空头期权的风险，市场「钉」在了行权价上。conversion 空着 call，reversal 空着 put，两种都有。The lecture's definition: pin risk is the risk of holding short at-the-money options at expiration, when the market is "pinned" to the strike. A conversion is short a call and a reversal is short a put, so both have it.

美国股票期权还有两条惯例让它更难办：收盘时实值 0.01 美元以上的期权默认自动行权；持有人在到期日 17:30（美东）之前还可以改主意，收盘后的盘后价格仍然会影响对手的决定。US stock options have two more conventions that make it harder: options in the money by $0.01 or more at the close are exercised automatically by default; and holders can still change their minds until 17:30 (ET) on expiration day, so after-hours prices still affect the other side's decision.

讲义的配套问答：恰好平值的期权，在到期那一刻理论价值是 0；有人还去行权它，理由是交易成本（行权换股票可能比在市场上买卖便宜）。所以对手会不会行权，没有理论答案。讲义给的是实务答案：不要把平值空头期权带到到期，收盘前把它买回来，代价大概是一个买卖价差。The lecture's companion Q&A: an option exactly at the money has a theoretical value of 0 at the moment of expiration; if someone still exercises it, the reason is transaction costs (exercising to get stock may be cheaper than trading in the market). 讲义还提到一个现象：临近到期，平值的 conversion 和 reversal 常按 C − P ＝ S − X 这个价成交（讲义叫 even money），持有的人借此把 pin risk 交给对手。讲义问：你该不该接？要看你手里的头寸和多数人一样还是相反。相反的话，接下这笔正好把自己的头寸平掉，自己的 pin risk 也没了，可以按 even money 接；一样的话，接过来等于同样的风险再加一份，要对方让价才值得接。The lecture notes also mention something else: near expiration, at-the-money conversions and reversals often trade at C − P = S − X (the lecture calls it even money), and the holders use this to hand their pin risk to the other side. The lecture asks: should you take it? It depends on whether your position is the same as most people's or the opposite. If it is the opposite, taking the trade closes out your own position and your pin risk goes away too, so you can take it at even money; if it is the same, taking it adds a second helping of the same risk, and it is worth taking only if the other side gives you a better price.

只有行权会产生标的头寸时才有 pin risk。现金结算的品种没有：SPX、NDX、RUT 这类指数期权，以及季月到期的股指期货期权，到期只按差额付钱，不会留下股票或期货头寸。Pin risk exists only when exercise produces a position in the underlying. Cash-settled products don't have it: index options such as SPX, NDX and RUT, and quarterly stock index futures options, pay only the difference at expiration and leave no stock or futures position.

六settlement risk：期货期权的 conversion 不中性6Settlement risk: a conversion on futures options is not neutral

股票期权的 conversion 是精确中性的。换成美国交易所的期货期权，就不是了。A conversion on stock options is exactly neutral. On futures options from US exchanges, it isn't.

先看股票期权。350 conversion 里，卖 call、买 put 合起来是合成空头，delta 是 −100（按一张合约 100 股计，相当于空 100 股）；一份股票 ＋100。合计 0。Start with stock options. In the 350 conversion, the short call and long put together are a synthetic short with a delta of −100 (counting one contract as 100 shares, equivalent to being short 100 shares); the stock is +100. Total 0.

换成期货期权：卖 1 张 call、买 1 张 put、买 1 张期货。2.3 讲过，美国的期货期权按股票型结算：期权买的时候全额付钱，盈亏到平仓或到期才兑现。可期货本身每天按结算价结清。讲义的问题：三月期货跌 10 点，期权上的盈利能不能正好抵掉期货上的亏损？Now futures options: sell 1 call, buy 1 put, buy 1 futures contract. As 2.3 explained, US futures options use stock-type settlement: options are paid in full when bought, and gains or losses are realized only when closed or at expiration. But the futures contract itself is settled daily at the settlement price. The lecture's question: if March futures fall 10 points, does the gain on the options exactly offset the loss on the futures?

  • 期货亏的 10 点当天就要从保证金账户划走，这是已实现的亏损，钱不够就得借。The 10-point futures loss is taken out of the margin account the same day. It is a realized loss, and if there isn't enough money you have to borrow.
  • 合成空头赚的 10 点要到期权到期才拿到，是未实现的盈利。The 10 points gained on the synthetic short are received only when the options expire. It is an unrealized gain.
  • 中间这段时间，借来的 10 点要付利息。In between, the borrowed 10 points cost interest.

反过来期货涨 10 点，账户当天收进 10 点现金，能生息。所以这个头寸总是希望期货涨：它带着正 delta。讲义给这个风险起名 settlement risk。If instead the futures rise 10 points, the account receives 10 points of cash that day, which can earn interest. So this position always wants the futures to rise: it carries positive delta. The lecture notes name this risk settlement risk.

买股票、卖 call、买 put。到期一定按 X 卖出股票。Buy stock, sell a call, buy a put. At expiration you are sure to sell the stock at X.
reversal
卖空股票、买 call、卖 put。到期一定按 X 买回股票。也叫 reverse conversion。Sell stock short, buy a call, sell a put. At expiration you are sure to buy the stock back at X. Also called reverse conversion.
combo
C − P，同一行权价的 call 减 put。等于按远期价算的内在价值的现值。C − P, the call minus the put at the same strike. Equals the present value of the intrinsic value at the forward price.
贷方 / 借方Credits / debits
收到的钱 / 付出的钱。推导 parity 时每一笔都带上到期前的利息。Money received / money paid. When deriving parity, each one carries its interest until expiration.
内在价值Intrinsic value
立刻行权能得到的钱，不够就是 0。The money from exercising immediately, or 0 if negative.
隐含分红Implied dividend
从期权价格反推出来的、市场认定的分红。The dividend the market assumes, backed out of option prices.
买一 / 卖一Bid / ask
市场上最高的买价 / 最低的卖价。卖出只能按买一，买入只能按卖一。The highest bid / lowest ask in the market. You can only sell at the bid and buy at the ask.
中间价Mid price
（买一 ＋ 卖一）÷ 2（3.1）。(Bid + ask) ÷ 2 (3.1).
delta / gamma / vega / rho
标的涨 1 美元仓位的盈亏（2.1）/ delta 的变化（2.2）/ 隐含波动率涨 1 个点的盈亏（2.3）/ 利率涨 1 个百分点的盈亏（2.3）。conversion 前三个都是 0，rho 为负。Position P&L when the underlying rises $1 (2.1) / change in delta (2.2) / P&L when implied volatility rises 1 point (2.3) / P&L when interest rates rise 1 percentage point (2.3). For a conversion the first three are 0 and rho is negative.
butterfly / iron butterfly
买 1 卖 2 买 1 三个等距行权价 / 买中间的 straddle、卖两翼的 strangle（3.2）。两者之和到期固定值行权价间距（5.1）。Buy 1, sell 2, buy 1 at three equally spaced strikes / buy the middle straddle, sell the outer strangle (3.2). Together they are worth the strike width at expiration, fixed (5.1).
期货Futures
约定到期按今天定下的价格买卖标的的合约，不付全款，盈亏每天用现金结清。A contract to buy or sell the underlying at expiration at a price agreed today; not paid in full, with gains and losses settled in cash every day.
保证金账户Margin account
交易期货时放在经纪商那里的钱，期货的盈亏每天从这里扣或存进来。The money held at the broker for trading futures; daily futures gains and losses are debited from or credited to it.
隐含波动率Implied volatility
从期权价格反推出的波动率（1.2）。本页的二叉树用报价快照给每个行权价算的值。Volatility backed out of option prices (1.2). The binomial tree on this page uses the value the quote snapshot gives for each strike.
二叉树模型Binomial tree model
把到期前的时间切成很多小步，每步标的涨一格或跌一格，从到期往回算期权今天的价值。美式 put 每一步都比较「现在行权」和「继续拿着」。Cut the time to expiration into many small steps, with the underlying moving up or down one notch each step, and work back from expiration to the option's value today. At every step an American put compares "exercise now" with "keep holding".
提前行权价值Early-exercise value
美式 put 比同样条件的欧式 put 多出的价值，本页用二叉树算。它让 C − P 偏低，从 parity 反解的利率随之偏低。The value an American put has over a European put with the same terms, computed on this page with the binomial tree. It makes C − P come out low, and the interest rate backed out of parity comes out low with it.
报价快照Quote snapshot
本页 TSLA 的真实买卖价、隐含波动率和未平仓量来自历史期权报价快照，按分钟记录。The real TSLA bids, asks, implied volatilities and open interest on this page come from historical option quote snapshots, recorded by the minute.
欧式 / 美式European / American
只能在到期日行权 / 到期前任何一天都能行权。parity 的等式按欧式推导。Exercisable only on the expiration date / exercisable on any day before expiration. The parity equation is derived for European options.
指派Assignment
卖出期权的一方被对手行权选中，必须履约。The option seller is picked when the other side exercises and must perform.
未平仓量Open interest
还没平掉的合约张数。The number of contracts not yet closed.
pin risk
到期时手里有平值空头期权，不知道会不会被指派的风险。The risk of holding short at-the-money options at expiration without knowing whether they will be assigned.
现金结算Cash settlement
到期不交割标的，按差额付现金。没有 pin risk。No delivery of the underlying at expiration; the difference is paid in cash. No pin risk.
已实现 / 未实现Realized / unrealized
已经变成现金进出账户的盈亏 / 还只是账面上的盈亏。P&L that has already moved in or out of the account as cash / P&L that exists only on paper.
股票型 / 期货型结算Stock-type / futures-type settlement
买期权时全额付钱、盈亏平仓才兑现 / 不付全款、每天按结算价结清（2.3）。Pay in full when buying the option, realize gains only on closing / no full payment, settled daily at the settlement price (2.3).
现值Present value
将来才收到的一笔钱今天值多少：除以 1 ＋ r·t。What an amount received in the future is worth today: divide by 1 + r·t.
合成 deltaSynthetic delta
合成股票（call 减 put）的 delta。股票期权里正好 100；股票型结算的期货期权里是 100 ÷ (1 ＋ r·t)。The delta of synthetic stock (call minus put). Exactly 100 with stock options; 100 ÷ (1 + r·t) with stock-type-settled futures options.
even money
按 C − P ＝ S − X 成交平值的 conversion 或 reversal，临近到期时常见。Trading an at-the-money conversion or reversal at C − P = S − X, common near expiration.
settlement risk
股票型结算的期货期权里，期货的已实现亏损要借钱垫付，期权的盈利到期才到，conversion 因此带正 delta。With stock-type-settled futures options, realized losses on the futures have to be funded with borrowed money while the option gains arrive only at expiration, so a conversion carries positive delta.
